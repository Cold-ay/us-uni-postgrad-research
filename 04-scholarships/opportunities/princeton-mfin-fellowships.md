# Princeton Bendheim Center MFin Partial Tuition Fellowships (Andlinger / Bendheim / Biglari) (SCH-086)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** fellowship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Select students; Statement of Financial Resources required at application
- **Scope:** princeton-msf
- **Programs:** princeton-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Partial fellowships in support of tuition offered to select MFin students each year. BCF Annual Report 2026 notes four fellows in 2025–26 across three endowed fellowships: Andlinger ’52 Graduate Fellowship in Finance, Bendheim Graduate Fellowship in Finance, Biglari Graduate Fellowships in Finance. Graduate School: Finance (Master's) applicants must complete Statement of Financial Resources; assistance awarded on academic merit, promise, and need. MFin is NOT on the Graduate School list of fully funded master's programs — expect primarily self-pay unless awarded.

## Eligibility
Admitted Princeton MFin students. International applicants use the same application; funding is not citizenship-limited in Graduate School general language. Amounts and selection criteria beyond 'select students' / merit+need not published as fixed packages.

## Amount
Partial tuition (amounts not published; not full funding)

## Deadline
With application (Statement of Financial Resources); Dec 15, 2026 app deadline for Fall 2027

## Notes
Central Graduate School master's funding page does not list Finance M.Fin. as a fully funded program.
