# Taiwan MOE Study Abroad Public Scholarship Exam (公費留學考試) (SCH-020)

- **Category:** National · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** 2027 quotas announced; brochure due H1 2027
- **Scope:** Field/country-specific quotas
- **Programs:** mit-cs, multi
- **HK eligible:** no · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** no

## Summary
Taiwan national government-funded study-abroad exam; 2027 exam: 83 fields, 201 places, 25 fields AI-related; switching to document review + interview.

## Eligibility
Taiwan nationals (details in 2027 brochure, due first half of 2027). 2026 trusted-industry fields (AI, ICT, cybersecurity, semiconductors etc.) were doctoral. Degree level for 2027 fields: unclear until brochure. Hong Kong: not applicable.

## Amount
2026 (115) cycle: tuition total cap US$120,000 over 3 years (trusted-industry fields); living allowance up to US$28,000/yr (e.g. NYC, Cambridge MA, SF, Stanford, Berkeley)

## Deadline
2026 cycle registration 15–31 Jul 2026; 2027 cycle dates unknown (brochure H1 2027)
