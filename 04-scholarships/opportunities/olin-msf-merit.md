# WashU Olin MS in Finance Merit Scholarships (SCH-087)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** washu-msf
- **Programs:** washu-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Every applicant admitted to Olin MSF is automatically considered for merit-based scholarship; no separate application. Same criteria as admission but more competitive. Award notified in writing at admission. Distinct from Olin Full-Time MBA merit/signature scholarships (SCH-052).

## Eligibility
Admitted Olin MSF students. Page does not restrict by citizenship. U.S. Armed Forces admits also considered for Olin Veterans Association Fellowships / Yellow Ribbon (US military — not intl).

## Amount
Not published (merit; notified at admission)

## Deadline
With admission (no separate application)
