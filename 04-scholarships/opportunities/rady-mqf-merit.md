# UC San Diego Rady MQF Merit Scholarships (SCH-114)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** ucsd-msf
- **Programs:** ucsd-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Rady provides limited scholarship support to exceptional individuals. All domestic and international admitted students are considered for merit-based scholarship upon admission; no additional application required. Donor-supported named fellowships (e.g., Brandes, Anklesaria, Fellows for the Future, Silberman, Alicia and Erwin Milan Family) may require completing the Financial Assistance section / short answer by the program Fellowship Deadline — several are framed for outstanding graduate students (some MBA-named awards are MBA-only; do not merge those).

## Eligibility
Admitted Rady MQF (ucsd-msf) students. Explicitly domestic and international. UC undergraduate pathway award is separate (SCH-115).

## Amount
Limited; amounts not published (except UC Scholarship pathway $10,000 — separate card)

## Deadline
With admission (named fellowships: Fellowship Deadline on application)

## Notes
Program renamed MFin → MQF; card attaches to catalog id ucsd-msf.
