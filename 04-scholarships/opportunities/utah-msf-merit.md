# Utah Eccles MSF Merit Scholarship (SCH-091)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Essay application after admission (new admits)
- **Scope:** utah-msf
- **Programs:** utah-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Admissions scholarship acknowledging academic, personal, and professional accomplishments. Potential award $0–$3,000. Separate 300–500 word essay to MSF@Utah.edu. Only one admissions scholarship per student. Funding limited; first-come first-serve. Requirements must be met before Nov 1 (spring) or June 1 (fall).

## Eligibility
Newly admitted MSF students who have not started the program. No citizenship restriction stated. UU employees on reduced tuition benefit ineligible. Program does not offer GA/fellowships.

## Amount
$0 – $3,000

## Deadline
Before Nov 1 (spring start) or June 1 (fall start); first-come first-serve
