# CSC National High-Level University Graduate Program (SCH-067)

Checked: 20 October 2026. PhD and joint PhD outbound only — not a general US MBA or terminal MS fund.

Mainland PRC nationals without foreign permanent residency. Living stipend plus one round-trip airfare; tuition is not covered.
