# Santa Clara Leavey Dean’s Merit Scholarships (MSFA) (SCH-121)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** With admission (must meet Apr 1 priority)

## Summary
Dean’s Merit Scholarships for exceptional academic and/or professional achievements for on-campus MSBA/MSFA/MSIS. Award range $1,000–$3,000. Applicants must apply by April 1 Priority Deadline.

## Eligibility
Admitted on-campus MSFA (and MSBA/MSIS) applicants meeting April 1 priority deadline.

## Amount
$1,000–$3,000

## Deadline
April 1 Priority Deadline

## Notes
Distinct from general merit SCH-120 range.
