# Harvard MBA need-based scholarship

Record: SCH-004. Checked 19 October 2026.

HBS provides need-based scholarship support to admitted domestic and international MBA students. It is program-specific and should not be assumed to fund Harvard CS or CSE degrees.

Apply for aid after admission. Individual eligibility, award value, deadline, renewal and stacking rules require confirmation. A published average award is not an applicant-specific estimate.
