## Summary
Jane Street fellowship for exceptional PhD students (or students committed to start PhD Fall 2027) in EECS, mathematics, physics, or statistics. International students explicitly encouraged.

## Eligibility
At least one year remaining in PhD (or committed to commence PhD Fall 2027) in listed fields; full-time at accredited research institution in US, Canada, UK, Europe, Australia, New Zealand, or Singapore. International students eligible and encouraged. Not for MBA/terminal MS.
