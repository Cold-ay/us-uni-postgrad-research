# Stanford ICME Limited MS Teaching/Course & Research Assistantships (SCH-147)

- **Category:** Assistantship · **Type:** assistantship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Competitive; not guaranteed; often via faculty/CA application after enrollment
- **Scope:** stanford-mcf
- **Programs:** stanford-mcf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
ICME does not offer guaranteed financial support for MS students. Limited TA/CA and occasional RA appointments may cover 8–10 units tuition plus salary at 50% time (20 hrs/week); reduced 25% appointments possible. MCF FAQ: admissions need-blind; no practice of linking guaranteed financial support to admission offers.

## Eligibility
ICME MS students (including MCF track) who secure competitive TA/CA or RA. RA typically via faculty contact; TA/CA via ICME CA application (SUNet). Open without citizenship lock on ICME funding page; federal loans US-only.

## Amount
Tuition coverage 8–10 units + monthly salary at standard assistantship rates (exact $ unpublished on ICME page)

## Deadline
After enrollment / faculty arrangement (not automatic with admission)
