# SCH-010 — AAUW International Fellowships

Checked: 20 October 2026. Status: **2027–28 application closed** (per AAUW page as of research date).

Women who are not U.S. citizens or permanent residents may apply for graduate study in the U.S., including first STEM master’s or doctoral programs. Computer Science is in scope; MBA programs are generally out of scope unless the degree is on the DHS STEM list. Master’s stipend: $20,000; doctorate stipend: $25,000 (verbatim). CGPA ≥ 3.5/4.0 and full-time enrollment are required.

Hong Kong and Taiwan passport holders may qualify if they meet the non-U.S.-citizen criteria; verify personally.
