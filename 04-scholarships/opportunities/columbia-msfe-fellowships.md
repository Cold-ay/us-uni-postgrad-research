# Columbia Business School MS Partial-Tuition Fellowships (incl. MSFE) (SCH-127)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Indicate interest on MS application

- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
CBS Academics Tuition and Financial Aid: limited number of partial-tuition fellowships to exceptional MS students based on academic excellence, personal background, and professional experience. No separate fellowship application; School identifies outstanding MS applicants. Must complete MS admission application by deadline and indicate desire to be considered for fellowships. Award notice via Admissions status-change email; renewability depends on program length. Only August-entry MS students are considered for merit-based fellowships in the first year. Student loans available to domestic and international MS students per same page.

## Eligibility
August-entry CBS MS applicants including MSFE who opt into fellowship consideration by the application deadline. Limited / highly selective.

## Amount
Partial tuition; amounts not published

## Deadline
MS application deadline (dossier cites ~Jan 28, 2027 — verify live); indicate fellowship interest on application

## Notes
Institutional Funding page language is largely MBA-framed — this card uses MS-specific Academics tuition/aid fellowship text. Assistantships generally awarded to doctoral students per MS costs page.
