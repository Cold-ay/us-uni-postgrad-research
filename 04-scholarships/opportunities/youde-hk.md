# SCH-012 — Sir Edward Youde Memorial Fellowships for Overseas Studies

Checked: 20 October 2026. **Hong Kong–specific** — confirm the current WFSFAA / SEYM Trust notice for the Fall 2027 cycle.

EducationUSA lists HK$300,000 per year for one year of overseas postgraduate study for outstanding Hong Kong (and Macau per listing) students expected to contribute to the HKSAR after graduation. Taiwan eligibility is not applicable for this fund.
