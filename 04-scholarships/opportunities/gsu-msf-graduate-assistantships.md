# Georgia State Robinson MS Finance Graduate Assistantships (SCH-088)

- **Category:** Assistantship · **Type:** assistantship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Auto-considered at admission; also post-admit Handshake applications
- **Scope:** gsu-msf
- **Programs:** gsu-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Graduate research/administrative assistantships offset MS Finance costs. Applicants with completed applications are automatically considered for GA awards at admission; others may apply via Handshake after deposit. For Finance (12-month) sample: GA tuition waiver $14,865.50 + stipend estimate $5,000; out-of-country remaining cost with stipend ≈ $29,513.50 (published sample; subject to change). F-1 students are eligible. Cannot be counted toward I-20 minimum funding.

## Eligibility
Admitted RCB graduate students; min 3.0 GPA; register 9 credits fall/spring (6 summer); U.S. work authorization — F-1 eligible (need SSN after hire). Priority GA consideration: apply by Round 2 (Jan 25, 2027) for Fall 2027. Intl needing visa must apply by Round 3 (Mar 15, 2027).

## Amount
Sample Finance 12-mo: waiver $14,865.50 + stipend ~$5,000 (published breakdown; varies by appointment)

## Deadline
Priority with Round 2 app (2027-01-25); positions also post near term start

## Notes
Evening Buckhead MSF — GA requires weekday daytime campus availability (8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m.), which may conflict with work schedules.
