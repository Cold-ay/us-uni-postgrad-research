# Georgia Tech MS-QCF Graduate Teaching / Graduate Assistantships (SCH-146)

- **Category:** Assistantship · **Type:** assistantship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Select students during program
- **Scope:** gt-qcf
- **Programs:** gt-qcf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
GTA (tuition waiver + monthly salary) and GA (hourly salary) awarded to select QCF students throughout the program. Program page: typically over half of students have some opportunity for funding at some point. Not an automatic admission offer; competitive/select.

## Eligibility
Enrolled MS-QCF students selected for GTA/GA. International students are not eligible for federal/state aid but assistantships are the primary institutional funding path described for the program.

## Amount
GTA: tuition waiver + stipend (amounts unpublished on QCF page); GA: hourly (unpublished)

## Deadline
During program (select awards; not a separate pre-admit open scholarship form on QCF funding page)

## Notes
SEPARATE TRACK quant-mfe. id=gt-qcf. Assistantship tuition uses reduced GA rate tables — not the full OOC sticker.
