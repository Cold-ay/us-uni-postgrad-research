# UT Dallas Jindal MS Finance Cohort Scholarships (SCH-104)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic consideration at admission (three levels)
- **Scope:** utd-msf
- **Programs:** utd-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Three levels of scholarship consideration available to high-achieving MS Finance Cohort students. Awards considered automatically at admission; applied toward tuition. Scholarships do NOT qualify students for in-state tuition. Cohort students are excluded from the general JSOM Graduate Scholarship portal (Dean’s Excellence / Impact / donor) — contact program director for options.

## Eligibility
High-achieving admits to the MS Finance Cohort. Page does not restrict by citizenship. Distinct from UTD Full-Time MBA Guaranteed Merit (SCH-057).

## Amount
Three unpublished levels (applied to tuition; does not create in-state rate)

## Deadline
With admission application (foreign applicants deadline Apr 30; domestic May 31 on cohort page)

## Notes
overlapsWithExisting SCH-057 is MBA cohort — do not merge. General JSOM competitive scholarship ≥$1,000 can unlock in-state for non-TX/intl, but MS Finance Cohort is excluded from that portal.
