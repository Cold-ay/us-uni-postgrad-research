# Utah Eccles MSF Community Impact Scholarship (SCH-092)

- **Category:** Student-Specific · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Essay application after admission (new admits)
- **Scope:** utah-msf
- **Programs:** utah-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
For candidates with exceptional leadership in public sector or non-profits / community impact. Potential award $0–$3,000. 300–500 word essay to MSF@Utah.edu. One admissions scholarship only per student.

## Eligibility
Newly admitted MSF students; community/public-sector leadership. No citizenship restriction stated.

## Amount
$0 – $3,000

## Deadline
Before Nov 1 (spring) or June 1 (fall); first-come first-serve
