# Taiwan MOE GSSA — 勵學優秀生 (low-income category) (SCH-019)

- **Category:** Need-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** 2027 cycle not yet published
- **Scope:** Any foreign university
- **Programs:** any US grad
- **HK eligible:** no · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** no

## Summary
Need-based category of the MOE study-abroad scholarship; master's OR doctoral allowed.

## Eligibility
Same base eligibility as GSSA plus valid municipal low-income or middle-low-income household certificate. Master's or PhD in any field (so MS CS / MBA eligible). 10 places (2026). Hong Kong: not applicable.

## Amount
US$30,000 per year, max 3 years (115/2026 brochure; same rate for indigenous and disability categories)

## Deadline
2026 cycle: 16 Jan–13 Feb 2026 (latest published); 2027 unknown
