# Villanova MSF Fellows Program (50% tuition + stipend) (SCH-102)

- **Category:** Assistantship · **Type:** assistantship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Separate fellowship application
- **Scope:** villanova-msf
- **Programs:** villanova-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Graduate assistantship: fellows work 10 hours/week in fall and spring assisting VSB faculty research, and serve as Admissions Ambassadors at select events. Previous employment must be terminated by program start. In exchange: 50% tuition discount (remission of tuition and fees) plus a living stipend paid bi-weekly over fall and spring. Cannot be combined with any VSB merit scholarships — choose one.

## Eligibility
Incoming Villanova MSF students who submit the MSF fellowship application. Explicitly available to international students per international students page.

## Amount
50% tuition discount + living stipend (stipend dollar amount not published)

## Deadline
Separate MSF fellowship application (see fellowships page / admissions)
