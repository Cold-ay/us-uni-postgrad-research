# SCH-011 — P.E.O. International Peace Scholarship (IPS)

Checked: 20 October 2026.

IPS supports women from outside the U.S. and Canada for graduate study in the U.S. or Canada, with intention to return to their home country. Maximum award: $12,500 (verbatim). Needs-based. Application windows are published on peointernational.org — verify the 2027–28 calendar before applying.

- [International Peace Scholarship Fund](https://www.peointernational.org/educational-support/international-peace-scholarship-fund/)
