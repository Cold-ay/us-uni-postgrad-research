# Taiwan MOE Government Scholarship to Study Abroad (GSSA 留學獎學金) — general (SCH-018)

- **Category:** Local · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** 2027 cycle not yet published
- **Scope:** Any foreign university (not mainland China/HK/Macau)
- **Programs:** mit-cs, multi (PhD only)
- **HK eligible:** no · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** no

## Summary
Taiwan Ministry of Education merit scholarship selected by document review; applicants must already hold an admission letter.

## Eligibility
ROC nationals with household registration in Taiwan; ≤45; admission from a recognised foreign university. General students: doctoral degree only (except arts/architecture/New Southbound countries). Hong Kong: not applicable. So MS CS / MBA NOT eligible under general category.

## Amount
General students: US$16,000 per year, max 2 years (115/2026 brochure)

## Deadline
2026 (115) cycle: 16 Jan–13 Feb 2026 (latest published); 2027 cycle unknown
