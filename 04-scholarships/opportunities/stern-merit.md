# NYU Stern Full-Time MBA Merit Scholarships (SCH-028)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** nyu-mba
- **Programs:** nyu-mba
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes

## Summary
Automatic merit consideration; ~20–25% of admitted receive awards.

## Eligibility
International applicants automatically considered. HK/TW: yes — basis: "Stern: international applicants automatically considered for merit scholarships" (https://www.stern.nyu.edu/portal-partners/financial-aid/deadlines-timelines/prospectivestudents/international-applicants).

## Amount
'About 20–25% of admitted full-time two-year MBA students receive merit-based scholarships; most are full- or half-tuition awards' (official Stern scholarships page); some awards cover one year or a fixed amount

## Deadline
With admission
