# Fulbright Taiwan (FSE) Graduate Study Award (SCH-017)

- **Category:** Local · **Type:** grant
- **Status (2026-10-20):** Closed for Fall 2027 (deadline 1 Aug 2026)
- **Scope:** Any US institution (IIE placement, up to 5) + Rice PhD partner
- **Programs:** multi
- **HK eligible:** no · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** no

## Summary
Taiwan Fulbright commission award for Taiwanese to pursue US master's or doctoral degrees; this is the Taiwan route of SCH-001.

## Eligibility
Physically present in Taiwan Aug 2026–Jul 2027; bachelor's by June 2027 and ready to enroll Fall 2027; iBT ≥80 / IELTS ≥6.5 (master's), iBT ≥100 / IELTS ≥7.0 (doctoral); GRE/GMAT. Fields incl. STEM and Business. Hong Kong: not applicable. Citizenship wording beyond residency: see page (unclear here).

## Amount
Monthly stipend of US$2,831 to US$4,300 for 12 months (renewable for 2nd year); one-time settling allowance US$3,000; up to 9 awards (All Institutions)

## Deadline
August 1, 2026 (Fall 2027 cycle — closed); next cycle unknown
