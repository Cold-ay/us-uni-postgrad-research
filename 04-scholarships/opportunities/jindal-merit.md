# UT Dallas Jindal Full-Time MBA Guaranteed Merit Scholarship (SCH-057)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** utd-mba
- **Programs:** utd-mba
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes

## Summary
Every admitted FT MBA receives a merit scholarship; recipients pay Texas resident tuition.

## Eligibility
International and out-of-state recipients receive resident tuition rates; GMAT/GRE required for scholarship.

## Amount
At least $1,000 (guaranteed) plus resident-rate tuition; cohort distribution 41% $10k–20k, 52% $21k–35k, 7% $35k+/full

## Deadline
With admission
