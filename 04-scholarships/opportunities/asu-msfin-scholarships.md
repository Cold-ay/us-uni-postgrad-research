# ASU W. P. Carey MS-FIN Scholarships (SCH-099)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Short application required after admission
- **Scope:** asu-msf
- **Programs:** asu-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
W. P. Carey awards a limited number of scholarships based on academic performance, leadership activities, community involvement, and future goals. Extremely competitive. A short application is required after admission has been awarded. Domestic and U.S. permanent residents must complete FAFSA. Page also lists graduate assistantships and loans as possible solutions; GA details are unit-posted, not MS-FIN-specific amounts.

## Eligibility
Admitted W. P. Carey MS-FIN students who complete the post-admit scholarship application. Citizenship not restricted in scholarship paragraph; FAFSA required only for domestic/PR. Distinct from ASU Full-Time MBA 'Scholarships for All' (SCH-031).

## Amount
Limited; amounts not published on MS-FIN page

## Deadline
After admission (short application); apply early in cycle for competitiveness

overlapsWithExisting SCH-031 is MBA-only — do not merge. CFA Program Awareness Scholarships (3/year) are exam-fee awards, not degree tuition — skipped.
