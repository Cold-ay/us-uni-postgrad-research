# Arizona Eller MSF Competitive Merit Scholarships (SCH-125)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Early applicants / with admission
- **Scope:** arizona-msf
- **Programs:** arizona-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Competitive, merit-based scholarships available for early applicants. Awards based on date of application and strength of incoming metrics (GPA, work experience, personal statement, resume). Finance Department notes very little resources for giving scholarships; admitted students may also apply via Scholarship Universe for internal/external matches. Citizenship not restricted on merit paragraph.

## Eligibility
Admitted Eller MSF students; early application improves odds. Dollar amounts unpublished.

## Amount
Limited; amounts not published

## Deadline
Early application preferred (Early Admission Oct 15 for Fall cycle)

## Notes
Department flags limited scholarship resources — amber tone.
