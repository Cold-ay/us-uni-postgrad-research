# Rochester Simon Full-Time MS Merit Scholarships (incl. MS Finance) (SCH-097)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** rochester-msf
- **Programs:** rochester-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Simon states more than 90% of incoming full-time MS students receive merit-based scholarship support. Admissions Committee determines awards when reviewing the MS application — no additional scholarship application. Award amount included with admission offer; amount is guaranteed and set aside for MS studies. Merit consideration available throughout all application rounds (funds diminish later but remain for highly competitive applicants).

## Eligibility
Admitted Simon Full-Time MS students including MS in Finance. Page does not restrict by citizenship.

## Amount
Not published (majority of class receives support; notified with offer)

## Deadline
With admission (no separate application)

## Notes
Partner-school guaranteed minimum $10k/year and UR undergrad 40% guarantee are US-pathway awards — see ingest:false cards. Teach For America $20k guarantee is TFA-specific — skipped as SCH.
