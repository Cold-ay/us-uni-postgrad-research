# Brandeis School of Business and Economics MSF Merit Scholarships (SCH-090)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** brandeis-msf
- **Programs:** brandeis-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
100% of admitted students automatically considered for partial and full-tuition merit scholarships (leadership, academic achievement, personal background, graduate education focus). FAQ: scholarships range from $5,000 to full tuition; notified in admission letter. Domestic and international students included. Merit awards apply to tuition only.

## Eligibility
Admitted Brandeis SBE MSF (and other SBE master's) students from the U.S. and around the world; automatic consideration.

## Amount
$5,000 to full tuition (FAQ); exact award in admission letter

## Deadline
With admission (apply early encouraged)

## Notes
A Fall 2026 'guaranteed $10,000 scholarship for all admitted SBE graduate students' appeared in search snippets of older admissions copy; live Fall 2027 pages fetched 2026-10-26 confirm auto merit $5k–full tuition but do NOT clearly restate a universal $10k guarantee — do not treat $10k as verified for Fall 2027.
