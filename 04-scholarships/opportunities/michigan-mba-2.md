# SCH-007 — Dean’s Impact Scholars / Dean's Fellows (full tuition)

Checked: 20 October 2026.

Highly select admitted Full-Time MBA students may receive full-tuition awards such as Dean’s Impact Scholars or Dean's Fellows. Dean’s Impact also cites an annual stipend for educational travel and leadership; the stipend amount is not specified on the page. International eligibility is unclear on the source page.
