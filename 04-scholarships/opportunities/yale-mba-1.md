# SCH-005 — Yale SOM MBA Scholarships (post-admission application)

Checked: 20 October 2026.

Admitted full-time MBA students complete a separate Scholarship Application via the Yale SOM portal. Awards are holistic and include some country-specific funds. International students are included per the official page; Hong Kong and Taiwan passport eligibility must be verified against personal facts.

Median scholarship for the Class of 2027 was $65,000 on the cost page — an aggregate statistic, not a guarantee for any applicant.

- [Scholarships at Yale SOM](https://som.yale.edu/programs/mba/affording-your-mba/scholarships-yale-som)
- [Affording Your MBA](https://som.yale.edu/programs/mba/affording-your-mba)
