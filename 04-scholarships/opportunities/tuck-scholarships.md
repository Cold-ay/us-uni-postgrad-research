# Tuck MBA Scholarships (SCH-026)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** dartmouth-mba
- **Programs:** dartmouth-mba
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes

## Summary
Discretionary merit+need scholarships; every admitted student incl. international automatically considered.

## Eligibility
International students included; need-based not guaranteed. HK/TW: yes — basis: "Tuck: every admitted student, including international students, automatically considered" (https://tuck.dartmouth.edu/mba/blog/financial-aid-faqs-international-students).

## Amount
$10,000 to full tuition (per Tuck FA FAQ for international students)

## Deadline
With admission
