# Illinois Tech Stuart Graduate Merit Scholarships (MS Finance) (SCH-116)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** iit-msf
- **Programs:** iit-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
All Stuart School of Business graduate students are automatically reviewed for merit-based scholarship awards at the time of admission. Award availability and amounts vary by program; amounts are tiered based on academic credentials. Awards prorated evenly across courses needed for the degree; deferrable up to one year. International students may also be awarded need-based aid during admission. University graduate scholarships page: ~30–40% of graduate students receive scholarships annually. Citizenship not restricted on Stuart merit language.

## Eligibility
Admitted Stuart graduate students including MS Finance. Beacon Education / Coursera enrollees not eligible.

## Amount
Varies / tiered; amounts not published on Stuart page

## Deadline
With admission (no separate application)

## Notes
Open external MSF (AMP Plus One is optional undergrad path, not required).
