# Villanova School of Business MSF Merit Scholarships (SCH-101)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Post-admit Scholarship Request Form
- **Scope:** villanova-msf
- **Programs:** villanova-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
VSB offers merit scholarships to academically and professionally accomplished students. After admission, applicants submit a Scholarship Request Form via the Application Status Page. Holistic review of rigor of coursework, undergraduate GPA, standardized test scores, and professional background. Funding limited and first-come/first-served within the cycle — apply early. Typically one-time awards. Cannot be combined with MSF Fellowship (SCH-102).

## Eligibility
Admitted Villanova MSF students who submit the Scholarship Request Form. International students are eligible for merit scholarships after admission (not eligible for federal aid).

## Amount
Not published (varies; notified after request review)

## Deadline
After admission via portal; funding limited — early rounds preferred (intl recommended Round 2 Jan 15)

## Notes
Fellowships cannot be combined with VSB merit scholarships.
