# SCH-003 — Stanford MBA need-based fellowship

Checked: 16 October 2026. Scope: Stanford MBA only.

[Official financial aid page](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/programs/mba/tuition-financial-aid).

Admitted MBA students with demonstrated financial need are eligible regardless of citizenship. Fellowships do not require repayment. Admission decisions precede the financial aid application, and students are expected to contribute income/assets.

User eligibility and award amount: not assessed. Application details, aid deadline and stacking with external awards: to verify after program selection. Do not transfer this MBA funding policy to Stanford CS or other degrees.
