# Society of Women Engineers (SWE) Collegiate/Graduate Scholarships (SCH-063)

- **Category:** Student-Specific · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** 2027–28 cycle: opens Dec 1
- **Scope:** Any ABET-accredited engineering/computing school
- **Programs:** multi (CS master's)
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes

## Summary
School-agnostic scholarships for women in engineering/computing incl. CS; master's-eligible.

## Eligibility
International graduate students may apply if enrolled/accepted at a school with ABET-accredited engineering, computing or technology programs; 3.0 GPA; full-time.

## Amount
unknown (varies by named scholarship)

## Deadline
Opens Dec 1, 2026; closes Feb 1, 2027 (Kaleidoscope interest page)
