# SCH-013 — Joint Japan / World Bank Graduate Scholarship Program (JJ/WBGSP)

Checked: 20 October 2026.

JJ/WBGSP funds citizens of eligible World Bank developing countries who hold unconditional admission to a **participating** master’s program. Benefits include tuition, monthly stipend, airfare, health insurance, and travel allowance (verbatim summary). Most catalog CS and MBA programs are not participating programs — verify the current participating-program list before planning around this award.

2027 application windows on the World Bank site: Window 1 Jan 18–Feb 26, 2027; Window 2 Mar 29–May 21, 2027. Eligible-country lists change; verify China/Hong Kong/Taiwan status in the current PDF.

- [JJ/WBGSP program hub](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/scholarships/jj-wbgsp)
