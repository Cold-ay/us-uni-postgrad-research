## Summary
Merit-based fellowship for outstanding Columbia Engineering master's students demonstrating academic excellence, financial need, and interest in applying technology/analytics to global challenges (logistics, infrastructure, data-driven decisions, sustainability, finance, AI). Particular consideration for connections to or interest in Lebanon's economic development. Selected fellows receive $50,000.

## Eligibility
Admitted/applicant Columbia Engineering MS students who apply for the award after submitting the Engineering application. Not citizenship-limited on the published description; Lebanon interest is a preference factor, not a hard nationality lock.

## Amount
$50,000

## Deadline
After Columbia Engineering application submission (see funding page for cycle details)
