# Emory Goizueta Robert W. Woodruff Scholarship (SCH-030)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** emory-mba
- **Programs:** emory-mba
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes

## Summary
Top merit award; other Goizueta merit awards partial/full tuition.

## Eligibility
International applicants eligible for Goizueta scholarships. HK/TW: yes — basis: "Goizueta: international applicants eligible for Goizueta scholarships (Woodruff criteria carry no nationality bar)" (https://goizueta.emory.edu/full-time-mba/two-year-mba/life/international).

## Amount
Full tuition and fees plus $15,000 annual stipend

## Deadline
With admission
