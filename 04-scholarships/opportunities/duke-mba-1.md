# SCH-008 — Fuqua Daytime MBA Merit-based Scholarships

Checked: 20 October 2026.

All Daytime MBA applicants are considered for merit-based scholarships; no separate scholarship application is required to be eligible. Award amounts are not published on the tuition and costs page. International students are included per source.
