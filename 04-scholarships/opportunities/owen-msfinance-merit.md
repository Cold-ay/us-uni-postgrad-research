# Vanderbilt Owen MS Finance Merit Scholarships (SCH-096)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** vanderbilt-msf
- **Programs:** vanderbilt-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
All MS Finance applicants are automatically considered for merit-based scholarship at admission. Awards based on academic achievement, potential for excellence in a management career, leadership potential, application strength, and potential to contribute to the Owen community. Scholarships cover partial tuition; amount announced at admission. Owen does not offer need-based scholarships.

## Eligibility
Admitted Vanderbilt Owen MS Finance students. Page does not restrict by citizenship. Distinct from Owen Full-Time MBA merit / Dean's Scholars (SCH-051).

## Amount
Partial tuition (amounts not published; notified at admission)

## Deadline
With admission (no separate application)
