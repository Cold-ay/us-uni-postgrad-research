# MIT Sloan Master of Finance Fellowship (SCH-085)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** fellowship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** mit-msf
- **Programs:** mit-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Limited fellowships for admitted MFin candidates of all citizenships and academic backgrounds. Established through a gift to MIT Sloan; no separate application. Recipients typically notified at admission.

## Eligibility
Admitted MIT Sloan MFin students. Applies to U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and international students. Automatic consideration based on academic records, personal achievements, professional promise.

## Amount
Not published (limited number)

## Deadline
With admission (no separate application)

## Notes
Distinct from MIT Sloan MBA fellowships (SCH-025). Partner nationality funds listed on same page (Aker/Norway, FGB/Spain, Instituto Ling/Brazil, Kennedy Trust/UK, Habib/Lebanon, Kuo Sharper) are NOT separate SCH cards here — nationality-specific; see brief.
