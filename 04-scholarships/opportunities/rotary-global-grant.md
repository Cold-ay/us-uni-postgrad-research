# SCH-014 — Rotary Foundation Global Grant Scholarship

Checked: 20 October 2026.

Global Grants support graduate study outside the applicant’s home country in a Rotary Area of Focus. Applications are club- and district-mediated; amounts and deadlines are not fixed on a single global page. Confirm with a local Rotary club in Hong Kong, Taiwan, or elsewhere before relying on this route.

- [Rotary scholarships hub](https://www.rotary.org/en/our-programs/scholarships)
