# Notre Dame Mendoza MSF Scholarships (SCH-103)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic consideration; complete scholarship section in application
- **Scope:** notredame-msf
- **Programs:** notredame-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Program-sponsored scholarships awarded via holistic review of the application. All applicants are automatically considered. Scholarship recipients expected to contribute actively to MSF and Mendoza communities. Scholarship decisions provided at admission. Complete the scholarship section within the application.

## Eligibility
Qualified MSF candidates regardless of citizenship (explicit on Mendoza MSF page). International applicants strongly encouraged to apply by Round 2 for visa timing.

## Amount
Not published (notified at admission)

## Deadline
With application scholarship section; rounds through Mar 17, 2027 (intl by Round 2 Jan 7, 2027 encouraged)

## Notes
Also mentions earned assistantships arranged with academic department (earnings for personal expenses; not a published MSF stipend package).
