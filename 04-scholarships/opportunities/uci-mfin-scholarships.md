# UC Irvine Merage Master of Finance Scholarships (SCH-100)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** uci-msf
- **Programs:** uci-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Merage School offers scholarships to help finance graduate business education. All applicants are automatically considered during admissions; no separate application. Holistic review may include academic achievement, professional experience, leadership potential, and overall application strength. Availability and award amounts vary by program and cycle. Recipients notified as part of admissions. Program overview notes a majority of students receive scholarship awards. Early applications generally improve scholarship chances.

## Eligibility
Admitted Merage Master of Finance students. Page does not restrict by citizenship. International students are separately directed to private loans/external scholarships for federal-aid gap.

## Amount
Varies (majority of students receive awards; dollar amounts unpublished)

## Deadline
With admission (Round 4 Apr 1 listed as scholarship priority deadline on admissions page)

## Notes
Round deadlines on Merage MFin admissions: R1 Oct 15, R2 Dec 1, R3 Feb 1 (intl recommended), R4 Apr 1 scholarship priority.
