# Hong Kong Scholarship for Excellence Scheme (HKSES) — postgraduate (SCH-015)

- **Category:** Local · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** Open · Fall 2027 cycle
- **Scope:** Any world-renowned university outside HK (US incl.)
- **Programs:** any US grad
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** no · **International:** no (HK residents only)

## Summary
HKSAR Government (EDB) scheme supporting outstanding local students to study outside Hong Kong; primarily undergraduate but postgraduate (master's/doctoral) applicants are eligible.

## Eligibility
Right of abode / right to land in HK (or entered on One-way Permit) AND resided in HK continuously for 3 complete years before programme start. Postgraduate applicants may hold undergraduate degrees from inside or outside HK. Programme must start Aug 2027–Jul 2028. Scheme 'will primarily benefit students pursuing studies at undergraduate level'. Taiwan: not applicable.

## Amount
Non-means-tested scholarship covering tuition 'subject to a ceiling of HK$300,000 per annum'; means-tested bursary 'subject to a ceiling of HK$200,000 per student per annum'. Master's: up to two years. Up to 100 offers per cohort.

## Deadline
6 pm (HKT) 31 December 2026 (2027/28 cycle)
