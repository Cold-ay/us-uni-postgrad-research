# SCH-006 — Michigan Ross Full-Time MBA Merit Scholarships

Checked: 20 October 2026.

Ross provides automatic merit scholarship consideration on Full-Time MBA admission. No separate application is required; students are notified upon admission. Published range: $10,000 to full tuition (verbatim on the affording page). International students are included per source; Hong Kong and Taiwan eligibility requires personal verification.

- [Affording Your MBA](https://michiganross.umich.edu/graduate/full-time-mba/admissions/tuition-financial-aid/affording-your-mba)
