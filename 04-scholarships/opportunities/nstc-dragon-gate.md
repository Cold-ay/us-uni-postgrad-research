# Taiwan NSTC Graduate Students Study Abroad Program (千里馬計畫) (SCH-021)

- **Category:** National · **Type:** grant
- **Status (2026-10-20):** 2027-departure cycle closed (Jul 2026)
- **Scope:** Research visit abroad for Taiwan-enrolled PhD students
- **Programs:** multi (research visit only)
- **HK eligible:** no · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** no

## Summary
NSTC partial grant for PhD students enrolled in Taiwan to do 7–12 months research abroad. NOT degree funding for US master's/MBA — included for completeness.

## Eligibility
Doctoral students in Taiwan universities. Hong Kong: not applicable.

## Amount
Partial support calculated at NT$900,000 per year, pro-rated by days abroad (7–12 months)

## Deadline
116 (2027) cycle: 1 Jun–31 Jul 2026 (closed)
