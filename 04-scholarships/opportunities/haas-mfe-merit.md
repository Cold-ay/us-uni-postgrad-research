# Berkeley Haas MFE Partial-Tuition Merit Scholarship (SCH-140)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** berkeley-mfe
- **Programs:** berkeley-mfe
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
All MFE candidates are considered for a partial-tuition Merit Scholarship with no separate application; typically offered with the admissions decision. Haas also lists donor-named awards (Matrosova Leadership, Arthur Qi Book $1,000, Mark Rubinstein Fellowship) and a general need-based scholarship via the scholarship portal aligned to admission rounds. Limited funding; earlier rounds advised.

## Eligibility
Admitted Berkeley Haas MFE students (FT or Flex). Page addresses scholarships to admitted students without citizenship carve-out on the merit auto-consider language; international students have access to limited scholarships (most aid is loans).

## Amount
Partial tuition (amount unpublished); Qi Book Award $1,000 when awarded

## Deadline
With admission (merit); donor/need apps aligned to admission round deadlines

## Notes
SEPARATE TRACK quant-mfe. Spring-only program. SIEML alumni full first-year fee coverage is pathway-only — see SCH-148 ingest:false. Veterans benefits separate.
