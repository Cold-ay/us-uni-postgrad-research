# CMU MSCF Merit Fellowship (SCH-141)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** fellowship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** cmu-mscf
- **Programs:** cmu-mscf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
All admitted MSCF students are automatically reviewed for an MSCF Merit Fellowship. Awards are partial; MSCF Fellowship awards up to $60,000 for the entirety of the program based on overall strength of the applicant relative to the pool and contribution to representation within the MSCF community. Fellowship page notes ~25% of incoming students receive fellowships.

## Eligibility
Admitted CMU MSCF students (Pittsburgh or NYC). Automatic consideration; no separate fellowship application. International students are not eligible for CMU need-based/federal aid but merit fellowship language is not citizenship-restricted on the MSCF tuition/aid page.

## Amount
Up to $60,000 for full program (partial; ~25% of incoming class per fellowship page)

## Deadline
With admission (automatic)
