# Paul & Daisy Soros Fellowships for New Americans (SCH-038)

- **Category:** Minority · **Type:** fellowship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** Open · deadline 29 Oct 2026
- **Scope:** Any US graduate program
- **Programs:** any US grad
- **HK eligible:** no · **TW eligible:** no · **International:** no (green card required)

## Summary
For immigrants and children of immigrants.

## Eligibility
Age ≤30; foreign-born applicants need green card (or qualifying DACA). HK/TW nationals on F-1: no; HK/TW-born US green-card holders: yes.

## Amount
Up to $90,000 over two years

## Deadline
Oct 29, 2026, 2:00 p.m. ET (2027 cycle)
