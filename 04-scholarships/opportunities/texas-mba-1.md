# SCH-009 — Texas McCombs Full-Time MBA Merit / Recruiting Scholarships

Checked: 20 October 2026.

McCombs considers all Full-Time MBA applicants for merit scholarships regardless of country of origin. Published range: $2,000 to full-tuition awards (verbatim); awards may include out-of-state tuition waivers. Deadlines are tied to admission timing rather than a separate open call.

- [Full-Time MBA tuition & financial aid](https://www.mccombs.utexas.edu/graduate/mba/full-time-mba/tuition-financial-aid/)
