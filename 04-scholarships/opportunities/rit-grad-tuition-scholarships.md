# RIT Graduate Tuition Scholarships (Finance MS) (SCH-123)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** rit-msf
- **Programs:** rit-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Considered for RIT graduate scholarships just by applying; no separate application. Typically 10–40% tuition scholarship range; amount varies by student and department. Offered to top candidates for academic excellence. Scholarship letter in admissions portal at acceptance. Not stackable with other RIT scholarships. Online tuition-rate programs excluded. International applicants not living in the U.S. may qualify for these (distinct from Master Up residency rule).

## Eligibility
Admitted on-campus Finance MS students (qualifying graduate programs). Scholarships cannot be combined with Master Up / Master Plan / Combined Accelerated.

## Amount
Typically 10–40% of tuition

## Deadline
With admission (rolling)

## Notes
Master Up (30%) is separate restricted card SCH-124.
