# Rochester Simon Evans Lam George Eastman Circle Scholarship (SCH-098)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Awarded at enrollment (pool and match)
- **Scope:** rochester-msf
- **Programs:** rochester-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Established through support of Evans Lam ’84S (MBA). Awarded to five outstanding full-time MS students each year at the time of enrollment via a pool and match approach.

## Eligibility
Outstanding full-time Simon MS students (includes MS Finance). Citizenship limits not stated on page.

## Amount
Not published (five awards per year)

## Deadline
At enrollment (no separate public application described)

## Notes
Named award distinct from general auto merit (SCH-097).
