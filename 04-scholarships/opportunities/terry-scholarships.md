# Georgia Terry Full-Time MBA Merit Scholarships (SCH-053)

## Summary
Merit scholarships at admission. NOTE: Terry's graduate assistantships (tuition reduced to $25/semester + stipend) are assistantships, not scholarships — labelled here only for context.

## Eligibility
International applicants automatically considered (international applicants page); Round 3 is final international deadline.

## Amount
Scholarships $2,000 to $20,000

## Deadline
With admission; international by Round 3
