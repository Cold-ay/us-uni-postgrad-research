# Boston College Carroll Full-Time MSF Merit Scholarships / Fellowships (SCH-107)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** bc-msf
- **Programs:** bc-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
All domestic and international applicants to the full-time MSF (and MSA/MBA) are considered for merit-based scholarships or fellowships. Award decisions communicated with admission. Scholarship amounts range from $7,500 to full tuition for exceptional candidates. For Full-Time MSF and MSA entering Fall 2026 class stats: more than 95% of class received merit scholarships/fellowships; awards disbursed once a semester for two semesters (Fall and Spring). Graduate Assistantships may be offered in combination with scholarships (stipend for research/admin). Includes Dean's Scholarships for exceptionally strong MSF candidates. Page does not restrict merit by Regular vs Quantitative Finance track.

## Eligibility
Admitted Carroll full-time MSF students (Regular or Quant/STEM track). Explicitly open to domestic and international applicants. Distinct from MBA-only named fellowships (GWIB, ROMBA, Weiss MBA/MSW preference).

## Amount
$7,500 to full tuition (class: >95% of FT MSF receive some merit; exact offer unpublished until admission)

## Deadline
With admission (no separate application)

## Notes
STEM designation is Quant track only — funding language is not track-limited. Do not merge with MBA ROMBA/GWIB. overlapsWithExisting: none BC-MSF-specific in SCH-015–106.
