# Forté MBA Fellowships (SCH-034)

- **Category:** Student-Specific · **Type:** fellowship
- **Status (2026-10-20):** School-awarded with admission
- **Scope:** Forté member schools (many catalog MBAs)
- **Programs:** multi (MBA)
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes

## Summary
Fellowships awarded by participating schools to candidates advancing women in business; no separate application at most schools.

## Eligibility
Open to candidates of all nationalities, genders and gender identities; must be admitted to a participating school. HK/TW: nationality not a bar (school-specific).

## Amount
School-dependent; unknown for US schools

## Deadline
With admission (school-specific)
