# Claremont Graduate / Drucker School Fellowships (incl. MS Finance) (SCH-109)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** With admission (department recommendation)
- **Scope:** cgu-msf
- **Programs:** cgu-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
CGU merit-based fellowships awarded on academic department recommendation. Offered based on enrollment in 12–16 units each semester; adjusted if enrollment changes; honored through required coursework if SAP maintained. Drucker School FAQ: disperses $500,000 annually in fellowships; 85% of Drucker students receive some level of fellowship. Priority application deadlines maximize fellowship consideration. Citizenship not restricted on fellowship pages.

## Eligibility
Admitted CGU / Drucker students including MS in Finance. Apply by priority deadlines for max fellowship consideration. FAFSA is for US federal aid (domestic).

## Amount
Not published per student (pool $500k/year school-wide; 85% of Drucker students receive some fellowship)

## Deadline
Priority deadlines with admission (Fall 2027 priority Feb 1, 2027)

## Notes
General CGU/Drucker fellowship — not an MBA-only card. Amounts unpublished.
