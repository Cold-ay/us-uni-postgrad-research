# Santa Clara Leavey MSFA Merit-Based Scholarships (SCH-120)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission (priority Apr 1)
- **Scope:** santaclara-msf
- **Programs:** santaclara-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Merit scholarships automatically considered at application. For on-campus MSBA/MSFA/MSIS beginning Fall 2026 awards page: awarded to applicants with GMAT/GRE at or above average (650 GMAT / 315 GRE); priority to April 1 Priority Deadline submitters. Scholarship range $2,000–$8,000. Majority of merit awards go to students who officially submit GRE/GMAT and apply by priority deadlines; test-waiver students risk reduced eligibility. Citizenship not restricted on MS scholarship section.

## Eligibility
Admitted on-campus MSFA students. Priority deadline April 1. Online MSFA awards not covered by this on-campus table.

## Amount
$2,000–$8,000

## Deadline
With admission; priority April 1 for Fall

## Notes
Page labels awards for students beginning Fall 2026 — confirm Fall 2027 cycle ranges when updated.
