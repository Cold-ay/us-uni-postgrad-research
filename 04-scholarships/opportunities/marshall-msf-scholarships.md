# USC Marshall MS Finance Scholarships (SCH-089)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission; limited
- **Scope:** usc-msf
- **Programs:** usc-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
Limited MSF scholarships for admitted students based on holistic assessment. No separate application. U.S. citizens and international students eligible. Apply Round 1 or 2 for priority scholarship consideration. Program itself does not offer RA/TA (students may seek on-campus after start).

## Eligibility
Admitted USC Marshall MSF students; international students explicitly eligible.

## Amount
Limited; amounts not published

## Deadline
With admission; priority Round 1–2

## Notes
Distinct from USC Marshall Full-Time MBA merit (SCH-056).
