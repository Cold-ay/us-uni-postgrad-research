# Ohio State Fisher SMF Graduate Assistantship (SCH-112)

- **Category:** Assistantship · **Type:** assistantship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic consideration (1 appointment/year)
- **Scope:** ohio-msf
- **Programs:** ohio-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
SMF awards up to one Graduate Assistant appointment each year. GA is 10 hours/week and provides a 50% tuition waiver plus a monthly stipend. All admitted applicants automatically considered; no separate application. Subject to annual financial-aid budget.

## Eligibility
Admitted Fisher SMF students; extremely limited (≤1/year).

## Amount
50% tuition waiver + monthly stipend (stipend $ not published on page)

## Deadline
With admission (auto consideration)

## Notes
Distinct from cash merit SCH-111.
