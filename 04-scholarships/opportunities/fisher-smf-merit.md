# Ohio State Fisher SMF Merit-Based Cash Awards (SCH-111)

- **Category:** Merit-Based · **Type:** scholarship
- **Status (2026-10-26):** Automatic with admission
- **Scope:** ohio-msf
- **Programs:** ohio-msf
- **HK eligible:** yes · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** yes · **PRC:** yes

## Summary
All admitted SMF applicants are automatically considered for merit-based scholarships; no separate application. Limited merit-based cash awards to select incoming students may range from $6,000 to $18,000. Recipients notified prior to admissions response deadlines. Apply early — Priority 1 (Dec 11, 2026) and Priority 2 (Feb 19, 2027) for merit consideration; funds limited.

## Eligibility
Admitted Fisher Specialized Master in Finance students. Page does not restrict by citizenship.

## Amount
$6,000–$18,000 (limited; select students)

## Deadline
With admission; priority deadlines Dec 11, 2026 and Feb 19, 2027 for aid consideration

## Notes
Published cash range — rare among Batch3 auto merits.
