# Taiwan MOE Subsidized Study-Abroad Loan (教育部補助留學生就學貸款) — LOAN (SCH-064)

- **Category:** Local · **Type:** loan
- **Status (2026-10-20):** Rolling (bank-administered)
- **Scope:** Recognised foreign master's/doctoral degrees
- **Programs:** any US grad
- **HK eligible:** no · **TW eligible:** yes · **International:** no

## Summary
LOAN, NOT A SCHOLARSHIP. Government-subsidised bank loan with grace-period interest subsidy.

## Eligibility
ROC nationals; family annual income ≤NT$2,000,000 (or ≥2 siblings studying abroad); MOE-recognised foreign degree. Grace-period interest: MOE pays full if family income ≤NT$1,140,000; half if NT$1.14M–1.2M. Hong Kong: no.

## Amount
Master's: total NT$1,200,000 (yr1 ≤NT$600,000); PhD NT$2,400,000. Master's term 12 years incl. 3-year grace

## Deadline
Rolling via participating banks
