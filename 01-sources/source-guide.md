# Sources and evidence policy

Initial checks: 16 October 2026. Follow each source through to the exact program, edition and reporting period when building a dossier.

| Source | Intended use | Limits / handling |
|---|---|---|
| [Times Higher Education subject methodology](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/world-university-rankings/world-university-rankings-subject-2026-methodology) | Computer science; business and economics; institutional research context | Subject indicators are recalibrated. Record edition; subject and overall editions may differ. Not evidence of individual master's outcomes. |
| [QS business and management subjects](https://www.topuniversities.com/university-subject-rankings/business-management-studies/2025) and [QS 2026 US subject overview](https://www.qs.com/insights/which-us-colleges-are-best-for-engineering-business-or-computer-science) | CS and information systems; business and management; discover candidates | Subject rankings span levels; distinguish MBA-specific rankings. The linked 2025 page explains this distinction; retrieve current exact tables before recording ranks. |
| [US News graduate schools](https://www.usnews.com/best-graduate-schools) | US graduate CS and relevant business degree rankings | Direct tables/methodology not verified in this pass. Log access restrictions; do not invent ranks or substitute undergraduate rankings. |
| [CSRankings](https://csrankings.org/) and [methodology FAQ](https://csrankings.org/faq.html) | Find CS faculty and institutions by research area and publication activity | Publication-based research signal; record selected years, geography and areas. Does not measure teaching, admissions chances or internship access. |
| [AACSB directory](https://www.aacsb.edu/accredited) | Check business-school accreditation and discover programs | Accreditation is distinct from program fit or career outcomes. |
| [EducationUSA graduate financing](https://educationusa.state.gov/your-5-steps-us-study/finance-your-studies/graduate) | International-applicant funding guidance and assistantship leads | Eligibility and actual award terms require the funding provider's current rules. |
| Official program, bursar and graduate-school pages | Admissions, costs, duration, curriculum and funding | Primary evidence for actionable facts. Verify exact campus, degree, student category and year. |
| Faculty/lab pages and recent papers | Specialization, methods, theoretical/applied orientation and current projects | Affiliation does not establish availability to supervise master's students or accept new students. Record access separately. |
| Official degree-specific employment/internship reports | Internship participation, employers, job outcomes and career support | Record cohort, denominator, response rate and international breakdown where available. Full-time job offers are not internship placement rates. |

## Internship assessment

Separate guaranteed placement (only if explicitly documented), required internship, optional internship/co-op, research placement, and career-service support. Record whether students source positions themselves, when the curriculum permits an internship, and whether outcomes include international students. Describe supporting evidence and uncertainty instead of labeling an internship "easy" based on prestige or location. Check applicable work authorization against government and university international-office sources once applicant status is known.

## Cost assessment

Record USD amounts, academic year, per-credit/per-term/per-year basis, required credits/terms, mandatory fees, insurance and living budget. Separate published figures from calculations. Do not multiply a nine-month budget into a full degree without checking summer costs and program length. Scholarships reduce net cost only when awarded or explicitly modeled as an uncertain scenario; loans are not scholarships.
