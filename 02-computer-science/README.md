# Computer science — ranked graduate departments

The April 2026 U.S. News list contains 33 departments through rank 30 because seven schools tie at 27. See the [complete list and provenance](../01-sources/us-news-april-2026.md).

Each school has a separate dossier under `programs/`. The website shows actual master’s routes and restrictions. A graduate department ranking is not a master’s-program ranking.

Important Fall 2027 distinctions: Princeton is not accepting master’s applications; MIT and Caltech lack a standard external terminal CS master’s; Harvard CSE is a related degree; Washington’s PMP cannot accept applicants requiring F-1 or J-1 visas. Maryland’s current CSMS application route needs to be distinguished from its CMSC catalog entry.

Compare thesis/research access, professional coursework, capstones and degree-specific funding separately. Faculty membership does not guarantee supervision, research assistantships or access to a lab.
