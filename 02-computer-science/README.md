## Carnegie Mellon MS in Computer Science — exploratory seed

- The [official program page](https://csd.cs.cmu.edu/academics/masters/ms-in-computer-science) describes a coursework requirement with optional research and a thesis option. Admission does not confer preferential treatment for a later PhD application.
- The [SCS cost page](https://www.cmu.edu/sfs/tuition/graduate/scs.html) lists 2026–27 reference master's tuition of **$62,200 per academic year**, or **$31,100 per fall/spring semester**. It explicitly says actual rates may vary by program. This is neither a verified MSCS-specific quote nor a Fall 2027 price.
- That cost page lists **$37,374** in additional annual budget items, including **$1,500** estimated federal loan fees applicable to borrowers. Do not treat every budget component as mandatory for every applicant.
- The [SCS master's comparison page](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/education/masters/programs-comparison) is a useful starting point for degree structures and internship timing; a general summer internship note does not prove MSCS placement or availability.
- Faculty specialties, faculty access, program-specific internship results, Fall 2027 admission deadline, ranking positions and master's funding: **not yet verified**.

Future research should distinguish broad CS, AI/ML, systems, security, HCI and other specializations based on the applicant's interests. Create one folder per exact degree using the program dossier template.
