## Academic direction

Campus MSCS is 30 hours with one required specialization (11 areas; specialization coursework 15–18 hours). Course, Project (9h), or Thesis (12h) options. Complete within six years; keep distinct from OMSCS.

## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — GRE not required for MSCS; if provided, scores will be considered. Especially helpful without relevant degree, >10 years out of school, or if concerned about application strength (CoC admission requirements).
- GMAT: not_required — Campus MSCS does not list GMAT; GRE optional only.
- TOEFL: required — min 100 — Department requires TOEFL ≥100 on older 0–120 scale; effective Jan 2026 new scale requirement 5.0. See Institute English Proficiency Requirements for who must submit.
- IELTS: required — min 7.5 — IELTS accepted: overall ≥7.5; section mins Reading 6.5, Listening 6.5, Speaking 6.5, Writing 5.5.
- English waiver: Per Institute English Proficiency Requirements (who is exempt); confirm on Grad Education English proficiency pages.
- GPA: recommended — minimum 3 — scale 4 — Desirable minimum GPA 3.0/4.0; most candidates score higher.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 3 — Three recommendation letters requested within online application.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Statement of purpose — Statement of purpose reviewed carefully (CoC admission requirements narrative); answer essay/SOP questions in application.
- Resume: not_stated — Resume/CV not explicitly enumerated on CoC MSCS admission-requirements page (SOP, LORs, tests, GPA highlighted).
- Transcripts: required — Official academic credentials/transcripts per Grad Education ready-to-apply guidance; scan officially issued documents.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not described on CoC MSCS admission-requirements page.
- Fee: $95 — waivers: Waivers for outstanding undergrad achievement / affiliated recruitment events; intl applicants must complete materials and English proficiency before waiver request. — As of Aug 1 2023: $95 domestic / $105 international per application (non-refundable). amountUsd reflects domestic; intl pay 105.
- Other: On-campus MSCS — distinct from OMSCS
- Other: Fall admit only; deadline Feb 1
- Other: Strong undergrad CS background including C programming highly recommended

Do not confuse with OMSCS. GRE optional≠waived. IELTS bar 7.5 is high. Confirm resume upload prompts in GradApp.
