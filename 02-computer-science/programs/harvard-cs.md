## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — SEAS CSE how-to-apply (updated 2026-09-04) and GSAS Engineering program page: General GRE and Subject GRE scores are optional for the upcoming cycle. No single factor used as a filter.
- GMAT: not_required — Pure SEAS CSE/Data Science master's do not use GMAT. SEAS FAQ notes GRE or GMAT is required only for Master of Design Engineering / MS-MBA Engineering Sciences (separate GSD/HBS applications)—not for CSE SM/ME.
- TOEFL: required — min 5 — GSAS English proficiency (Fall 2027): non-native speakers without an undergraduate degree from an English-primary-instruction institution must submit TOEFL iBT. Minimum overall 5 (95 for tests taken prior to 2026-01-21) and Speaking 4.5 (23 prior to 2026-01-21). ETS code 3451; dept 99. Duolingo not accepted. Master's degree alone not accepted as proof.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS Academic minimum overall 7 and Speaking 6.5 (GSAS). Send via IELTS E-Delivery to Harvard Griffin GSAS. Home/online Academic versions accepted.
- English waiver: Undergraduate degree from an institution where English is the primary language of instruction. US institutions where English was not primary but a significant portion was in English may petition GSAS (US institutions only). SEAS FAQ: no waivers for the English Language Proficiency Requirement beyond GSAS pathways. Scores for Fall 2027 must be from tests taken no earlier than 2025-01-05.
- GPA: not_stated — SEAS CSE how-to-apply: no GPA cutoff / no minimum GPA requirement. Holistic review.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 3 — Three letters required from faculty, advisors, and/or employment supervisors who can speak to graduate-study ability; no more than four. Register recommenders in GSAS application; Interfolio not accepted. Late letters may be added but review begins after Dec 1.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as statement of purpose — About 1,000 words covering experiences (including training not on transcript), interests, and future goals.
- Resume: required — CV/resume covering education, work experience, honors, awards, papers, and other achievements.
- Transcripts: required — Upload electronic official or unofficial transcripts; do not mail hard copies. Fall grades can be updated after submission via Fall Grade Report.
- Interview: not_used — SEAS CSE how-to-apply: interview not required and in-person interviews with admissions staff are not offered.
- Fee: $105 — waivers: Fee-waiver eligibility determined by answering questions in the Application Fee section of the GSAS application; immediate system response. — Harvard Griffin GSAS application fee $105 payable by credit card (non-refundable).
- Other: FLAG: Harvard CS PhD page confirms no standalone CS master's; related terminal master's are Computational Science and Engineering (SM/ME) and Data Science—do not treat as MS CS.
- Other: Fall Term matriculation only; CSE/Data Science portal open for Fall 2027; GSAS deadline December 1, 2026.
- Other: Select Engineering and Applied Sciences → Computational Science and Engineering → SM or ME.
- Other: Do not apply to both Data Science and CSE.
- Other: No formal prerequisites; successful applicants show calculus, linear algebra, DEs, probability/stats, programming (e.g. Python/R), and basic CS concepts.
- Other: Master's candidates not eligible for SEAS financial aid.

Related CSE SM/ME for Fall 2027 (as_of 2026-10-27). Prefer how-to-apply + GSAS English/fee pages over CS PhD curriculum page. TOEFL uses new 0–6 scale minima for tests on/after 2026-01-21.
