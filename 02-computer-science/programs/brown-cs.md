## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — GRE General not required but score submission is encouraged. GRE Subject not required. If supplied, strong results expected (CS applications page).
- GMAT: not_required — Pure CS ScM; GMAT not part of program requirements.
- TOEFL: required — min 105 — Required for intl applicants whose native language is not English unless exempt. TOEFL iBT on/before Jan 20 2026: min 105. On/after Jan 21 2026: min 5.0 (5.5+ strongly recommended). No MyBest/Superscores. Code 3094.
- IELTS: required — min 8 — IELTS minimum overall 8.0 per CS ScM program page (above general master’s recommended 7).
- English waiver: Not required if degree from institution where English is sole language of instruction, or from listed countries (Australia, Bahamas, Botswana, Cameroon, Canada except Quebec, Ethiopia, Ghana, Ireland, Kenya, Lesotho, Liberia, Malawi, New Zealand, Nigeria, Zimbabwe, South Africa, Sierra Leone, Swaziland, Tanzania, Gambia, Uganda, UK, West Indies, Zambia).
- GPA: not_stated — No numeric minimum GPA on CS ScM graduateprograms page; seeks outstanding students with CS/equivalent background.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 3 — 3 letters for external applications; 2 for Brown fifth-year. Recent grads (≤3 years) / current students: at least 2 from professors who can speak to CS coursework.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Personal Statement / program questions — Master’s application process requires Personal Statement and/or short-answer/program questions (see application).
- Resume: required — Resume/CV required (CS ScM application requirements).
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial OK for application; official only for enrolling students. Non-U.S. degrees: WES/ECE/The Evaluation Company evaluation required before enrollment. Undergrads: include Fall grades when available.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not described on CS ScM program or CS applications pages.
- Fee: waivers: Need-based waiver via form in application; veterans/active-duty military eligible with documentation (Master’s Programs FAQ). — Dollar amount not stated on CS ScM or CS applications pages as of scrape; confirm in online application.
- Other: On-campus ScM (Comprehensive) and AI/ML track (campus or online) — same degree family
- Other: Fall deadline Jan 15 (intl & final); Spring Nov 1 (intl Oct 1); fifth-year May 1 Fall / Nov 1 Spring
- Other: Prereq-style background: intro programming, algorithms/DS, systems or SE, discrete math/probability, linear algebra + one advanced CS course

GRE optional but encouraged. IELTS bar is high (8.0). Distinguish on-campus Comprehensive vs AI/ML track. Fee amount confirm in portal.
