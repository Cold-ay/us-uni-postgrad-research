## Tuition and application timing

$52,950 — Academic-year tuition (SEAS on-campus master’s, 3 CUs × 2 semesters); 2026–27.

2026–27 reference, not a Fall 2027 quote or full-degree total.

Penn SRFS SEAS on-campus master’s 2026–27: tuition $8,825 per course unit (CU). Catalog CIS MSE requires 10 CUs; sample plans use 3 CUs in a typical Fall or Spring term → AY tuition reference $8,825 × 3 × 2 = $52,950. At 4 CUs/term: $35,300/semester ($70,600 AY). Mandatory fees at 3 CUs: General Fee $2,134 + Technology Fee $405 + Clinical Fee $385 (semester totals with tuition $29,399)—fees not included in the tuition figure. Full 10-CU degree tuition at $8,825 = $88,250. Online Engineering rates differ.

Application timing: Apps open 15 Sep 2026 · Early 1 Nov 2026 · 11:59 p.m. EST · Regular 1 Feb 2027 · 11:59 p.m. EST (CIS MSE Fall 2027). Confirm deadline time zone and personal requirements on the official page.

## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — Penn Engineering: GRE optional for current master's cycle (Fall 2027 open Sep 15, 2026–Feb 1, 2027). Omitting does not hurt; valid scores encouraged if available. Institution code 2888; scores valid 5 years; official scores by deadline.
- GMAT: not_required — Pure CIS MSE; GMAT not listed on Penn Engineering How to Apply.
- TOEFL: required — International / non-US citizen/PR whose native language is not English must submit TOEFL iBT (code 2888), IELTS, or DET. No hard minimum for admission; recommended TOEFL 5.0 (or 100 legacy 0–120). At-home TOEFL accepted. Scores valid 2 years; exam by deadline.
- IELTS: required — IELTS accepted; recommended 7.5. Send electronically to “University of Pennsylvania School of Engineering and Applied Science.” At-home IELTS accepted.
- English waiver: Waived if by matriculation applicant: graduates from a U.S. program; attends school in a country where English is official language; or graduates from an institution where English is the medium of instruction (indicated on unofficial transcript). Waiver processed after submit (3–5 business days).
- GPA: not_stated — No admission minimum GPA stated on Penn Engineering How to Apply. CIS MSE expects strong undergrad math/CS preparation (OS, PL, DS, discrete math, linear algebra, algorithms/theory).
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two letters for master's (three only for CIS Ph.D. / BE Ph.D. Medical Physics). Include one from a faculty member familiar with scholarly abilities. Submitted via application email link.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Personal Statement — Personal statement ≤2 pages answering: interest in program; candidacy; benefit; contribution to Penn Engineering community; reasons for success; post-completion plans.
- Resume: required — Professional resume covering job skills, depth of professional experience, leadership.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial transcript from each university/college with course credit; English with grade key; name, institution, courses, credit hours, grades. Official transcripts only if admitted and enroll.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not mentioned on Penn Engineering How to Apply.
- Fee: $95 — waivers: Auto-waived if US citizen/PR with proven low-income; Penn or U.S. military affiliation; or eligible US partner-institution Accelerated Master’s applicant. — $95 nonrefundable with online application.
- Other: Distinct from MCIT (conversion); non-CS undergrads directed toward MCIT/MAS-CS routes.
- Other: Fall 2027 master's: early CIS deadline Nov 1, 2026 11:59pm EST; regular Feb 1, 2027; decisions by Apr 1.
- Other: DET accepted (recommended 135).

Apply via Penn Engineering Grad Admissions (not catalog curriculum page). Early Nov 1 for CIS if seeking early review. GRE optional≠waived. Confirm Fall 2027 fee/waiver categories on live how-to-apply.
