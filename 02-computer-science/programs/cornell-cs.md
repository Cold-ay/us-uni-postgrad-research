## Academic direction

Research MS is a selective four-semester (≈2-year) degree: 34 credits with 6–12 thesis credits, an outside minor, and a written thesis + oral. Professional MEng is a separate ~1-year coursework + capstone route.

Orientation: MS research thesis · or MEng professional · Distinct routes

## Tuition and application timing

$29,500 — Academic-year tuition (Endowed research master’s MS); 2026–27.

2026–27 reference, not a Fall 2027 quote or full-degree total.

Bursar: Endowed Research Master’s (M.S. and listed M.A./non-degree) $14,750/semester, $29,500/year (2026–27). ## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: not_accepted — MS Apply: submitted GRE scores are masked and will not be reviewed. MEng Apply: GRE no longer required; submitting scores has no impact on decision.
- GMAT: not_accepted — CS MS/MEng do not use GMAT.
- TOEFL: required — Intl applicants must meet Grad School section mins (new scale): Speaking ≥5.5, Reading ≥4, Listening ≥3.5, Writing ≥4. CS MS and CS MEng both require these section minimums. Institution 2098; no dept code. Only TOEFL/IELTS accepted.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — Overall band ≥7.0 required (MS and MEng apply pages).
- English waiver: See Cornell Graduate Admissions English Language Proficiency Requirements for exemptions and timelines.
- GPA: not_stated — No numeric minimum on CS MS/MEng apply pages. Early MEng Admit pathway notes cumulative undergrad GPA ≥2.7 minimum to apply (Cornell undergrads only).
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 3 — MS: 3 letters including ≥1 addressing expository skills and classroom/TA ability. MEng: 2 letters (recent grads: ≥2 academic; current Cornell CS: 1 letter + filler). Dual-route note: counts differ by program.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Essay / Statement of Purpose / Personal Statement (MEng) — MS: essay explaining how degree and anticipated thesis area fit career plans. MEng: Statement of Purpose ≤1000 words (interests, why Cornell CS MEng, qualifications, goals) plus Personal Statement ≤1000 words.
- Resume: required — Required for CS MEng (explicit on MEng Apply). CS MS Apply page does not list resume; MEng path requires current resume/CV.
- Transcripts: required — Upload transcripts after latest term grades posted; official electronic transcripts before matriculation (Cornell undergrads exempt from that step).
- Interview: unknown — Interview not described on CS MS or MEng apply pages.
- Fee: $105 — waivers: MEng Duffield guide: application fee waivers generally not available for MEng applicants. Confirm MS Grad School waiver policy separately. — $105 online at submit (Cornell MEng application guide; standard Grad School fee for Ithaca graduate applications).
- Other: TWO ROUTES: Research MS (4 semesters, TA-funded, strong preference Cornell undergrads with excellent TA/research record; deadline Jan 18 Fall) vs professional MEng (~2 semesters, self-funded; Fall Feb 1 / Spring Oct 1)
- Other: MS is not a stepping stone to Cornell CS PhD
- Other: Prereqs typically CS 2110/3110/2800/3410 equivalents

External industry-oriented applicants usually target MEng. Research MS is small and prefers Cornell undergrads with TA excellence. GRE not considered.
