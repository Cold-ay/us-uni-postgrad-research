## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: not_required — EECS Graduate Admissions Information Letter and OGE program page: department does NOT require GRE scores for admission.
- GMAT: not_required — Not used for EECS PhD admissions.
- TOEFL: required — min 100 — International students: TOEFL iBT minimum 100; institute code 3514; department code 78 or 66. OGE: IELTS preferred over TOEFL; waiver may be available.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS minimum 7; electronic scores to MIT Graduate Admissions. EECS letter: IELTS can be used if TOEFL unavailable; same waiver cases as TOEFL.
- English waiver: EECS letter: TOEFL/IELTS may be waived e.g. if applicant has been in the U.S. at least two years, or if country's first language is English. OGE notes waiver of TOEFL/IELTS may be available.
- GPA: not_stated — EECS letter: admission influenced by GPA but there is not an official cut-off.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 3 — Three letters of recommendation required (EECS letter and OGE application requirements).
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as statement of objectives; essays — OGE: Essay 1 research activities and goals; Essay 2 academic and intellectual journey. EECS letter refers to Statement of Objectives detailing research interests.
- Resume: not_stated — Resume/CV not explicitly listed on OGE EECS application-requirements block reviewed (online application, two essays, three LORs, transcripts, English scores).
- Transcripts: required — Transcripts required; admitted students submit official transcript(s) to EECS Graduate Office. Do not send published papers or theses with application.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not stated on OGE EECS program page or EECS admissions information letter reviewed.
- Fee: $90 — waivers: Applicants can request a fee waiver via OGE eligibility criteria / EECS fee-waiver link. — OGE EECS listing: application fee $90.00.
- Other: FLAG — NO EXTERNAL TERMINAL MASTER'S: Department does not offer admission for a terminal master's. Apply for PhD; SM earned en route if needed. MEng degrees restricted to MIT undergraduates only.
- Other: Application opens Sep 15; deadline Dec 1 11:59 p.m. Eastern; Fall Term (September); Summer only for LGO.
- Other: Extremely competitive; strong background in math, physics, engineering, or CS expected.
- Other: PhD students typically supported by fellowship/RA/TA (tuition, stipend, medical insurance).

Flag as PhD-only / no external MS for Fall 2027 slate. Requirements above are for the EECS PhD application path only.
