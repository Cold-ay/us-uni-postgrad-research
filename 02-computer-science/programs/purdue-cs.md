## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: not_required — CS Admissions: GRE is NOT required. FAQ: GRE scores are not required by the CS Admissions Committee.
- GMAT: not_required — Pure CS MS; GMAT not listed among CS application materials.
- TOEFL: required — min 80 — Intl applicants whose native language is not English must demonstrate proficiency by deadline. University OGSPS minimums: TOEFL iBT total 80 (W18/S18/L14/R19) for exams before Jan 21, 2026; after that date overall 4.0 with section floors W4.0/S3.5/L3.5/R4.0. CS recommends competitive TOEFL 100 with no subscale <22 (legacy) or no subscale <4.5 (new scale). Code 1631 dept 78. Self-report + official ETS.
- IELTS: required — min 6.5 — University IELTS Academic minimum overall 6.5 (R6.5/L6.0/S6.0/W5.5). CS recommends overall ≥7.5 with no band <7.0 for competitiveness.
- English waiver: Grad School may waive TOEFL/IELTS (not TA speaking) if baccalaureate/graduate/professional degree within 36 months before admission from English-primary-instruction school in English native/official language country. CS notes submitting scores still helps funding/GTA. Duolingo accepted for Grad School admission (CS expects overall ≥135, no sub-score <135) but not for GTA eligibility.
- GPA: recommended — minimum 3.5 — scale 4.0 — Most successful applicants have major and cumulative GPAs above 3.5/4.0 (or 8.5/10 or 85/100). Institution quality considered. No absolute floor stated beyond holistic review; four-year bachelor’s (or equivalent) required before enrollment term.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 3 — At least three letters submitted electronically by application deadline. Prefer academic/professional references able to comment on CS graduate readiness; research supervisor letter expected if applicable. MS: prefer junior/senior year professors.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Academic Statement of Purpose (+ Personal History Statement) — Academic Statement of Purpose (plus Personal History Statement) listed in CS application steps and admissions criteria.
- Resume: required — Resume listed among CS Prepare your documents steps and admissions criteria.
- Transcripts: required — Transcripts required; upload per Grad School rules. Also CS Supplementary Form (PDF).
- Interview: unknown — Interview not mentioned on CS requirements/steps pages.
- Fee: $75 — waivers: Domestic hardship and listed recruitment-program waivers per Purdue OGSPS How to Apply; currently enrolled Purdue students/alumni often fee-exempt. — Purdue graduate application fee $60 domestic / $75 international (USD); one fee covers up to two campus/major selections. amountUsd records international rate.
- Other: Fall 2027 CS deadline Dec 1, 2026; Spring 2027 Oct 1, 2026.
- Other: CS Supplementary Form + Academic SoP + Personal History Statement required.
- Other: Applicants from another CS grad program normally complete ≥1 year there; if master's, normally complete that degree before Purdue registration.

Curriculum page is not the admissions checklist—use CS graduate/admission/*. GRE not required ≠ waived. Aim CS-competitive English scores if seeking GTA.
