## Academic direction

45-unit MS CS with Foundations, SSI, Breadth, Depth, and Electives on an approved program sheet. Nine predefined specializations (plus rare Individually Designed). Up to three years / nine non-summer quarters.

## Tuition and application timing

$69,717 — Academic-year tuition (Engineering, 11–18 units × 3 quarters); 2026–27.

2026–27 reference, not a Fall 2027 quote or full-degree total.

Graduate School of Engineering quarterly rate for 11–18 units is $23,239 (2026–27). Three academic-year quarters (Autumn–Spring) = $69,717. 8–10 units is $15,100/quarter. Per-unit above 18: $1,549. MS CS requires 45 units; HCP/online uses a different per-unit schedule. Living costs and Cardinal Care are separate.

Application timing: Apps open 15 Sep 2026 · Deadline 8 Dec 2026 · 11:59 p.m. PT (Autumn / Fall 2027 entry). Confirm deadline time zone and personal requirements on the official page.

## Funding

CS MS pages and Stanford Grad Aid: no guaranteed department funding for MS; international students generally ineligible for Stanford/federal loans. Course/Research Assistant roles exist but are limited; PhD priority. Knight-Hennessy (SCH-002) listed in catalog but 2027 application closed.
