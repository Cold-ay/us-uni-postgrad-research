## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — Official GRE optional for MS; choosing not to submit will not harm chances. ETS code 3917. UMass graduates exempt from GRE requirement.
- GMAT: not_required — GMAT not part of CICS MS admissions materials.
- TOEFL: required — min 80 — Graduate School minimum: TOEFL combined 80 (tests before Jan 2026) or 4.0 (tests Jan 2026 or later). CICS how-to-apply defers to Grad School International Applicants page. Code 3917.
- IELTS: required — min 6.5 — IELTS Academic minimum combined 6.5 (Graduate School). DET 115 and PTE Academic 53 also accepted.
- English waiver: May be waived for citizenship in listed English-speaking countries; bachelor's/master's from U.S. or listed countries; certain American University campuses; or currently enrolled full-time completing two academic years of U.S. college coursework before entrance (see Grad School International Applicants).
- GPA: required — minimum 2.75 — scale 4.0 — Graduate School minimum undergraduate GPA 2.75/4.00; programs may expect higher. CICS emphasizes strong CS undergraduate preparation (listed core CS/math courses).
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two letters; choose writers who can speak to research, community service, or work experience. Requested via Grad School application after submit.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Personal statement — Personal statement; suggestions include research/industry experience, computing for common good, and diversity/STEM impact. Grad School also requires a Short Essay (≤600 words) on community/group contribution.
- Resume: required — Resume/CV required for MS application.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial for application review; official required by Graduate School if admitted. Missing transcripts block evaluation.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not listed on CICS on-campus MS How to Apply page.
- Fee: $90 — waivers: Current Five College undergraduates applying to master's-only programs do not pay admission fee; other Grad School waiver categories (McNair, GRE fee reduction, etc.). — Nonrefundable $90 application fee payable online by credit card (UMass Graduate School).
- Other: Target on-campus MS (not online MS) for this catalog id.
- Other: Terminal MS students ineligible for campus assistantships (Fall 2016+ cohort rule); grader/hourly roles possible after first semester.
- Other: Undergraduate preparation checklist of CICS/MATH core courses strongly expected.

Use CICS How to Apply — MS Program (on Campus); degree-requirements page alone lacks admissions checklist.
