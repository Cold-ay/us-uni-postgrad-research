## Tuition and application timing

$68,670 — Academic-year tuition (WSE residential master’s / PhD full-time); 2026–27.

2026–27 reference, not a Fall 2027 quote or full-degree total.

Johns Hopkins Whiting School of Engineering Cost of Attendance: WSE Residential Master’s and PhD Full-time Tuition $68,670 annually / $34,335 per semester (2026–27). The separately listed “Non-resident Fee” $6,868 annually is a part-time/non-resident enrollment status charge, not an out-of-state surcharge for on-campus full-time students—do not add it to the residential full-time figure. ## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — MSE CS admissions: GRE not required; listed as optional material. FAQ also states GRE scores are not reviewed by CS / not included in admissions review—optional≠waived; submitting may have no effect.
- GMAT: not_required — Not used for Whiting MSE CS; only GRE discussed as optional/not reviewed.
- TOEFL: required — min 100 — International / non-native English speakers: TOEFL ≥100 (ITP Level 1 and iBT Home Edition accepted). Institution code C559. Scores older than 2 years not accepted.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS ≥7.0. Send to Johns Hopkins University, Whiting School of Engineering. Duolingo English Test ≥135 also listed.
- English waiver: See Whiting English proficiency waiver eligibility page linked from MSE FAQ. All non-native English speakers must take TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo per FAQ.
- GPA: not_stated — No numeric minimum GPA stated on MSE CS admissions page; holistic review. Background equivalent to BS CS expected.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two letters required via online application; only two guaranteed to be read regardless of how many submitted.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as statement of purpose — Required; upload via online application (file ≤500KB, ~1–2 pages, 1-inch margins per FAQ).
- Resume: required — Resume/CV required among application materials.
- Transcripts: required — Upload transcript copies for review; official transcript requested upon admission.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not listed among required MSE CS application materials on admissions or FAQ pages reviewed.
- Fee: $0 — waivers: Whiting School $75 fee waived for Fall 2027 master's applicants; admissions page: Apply free / no application fees required. — Applications free for Fall 2027 MSE CS (fee waived).
- Other: Fall 2027 deadline Feb 15 (11:59 p.m. EST); Spring 2027 Oct 1.
- Other: Sample of work optional.
- Other: Non-CS backgrounds need intermediate programming (C++ and Java), data structures, computer-system fundamentals, and mathematical foundations (coursework/research/work).
- Other: HLT concentration and AI track available; distinct from Engineering for Professionals evening/online MS.

Prefer MSE Admissions + FAQ over program-requirements/curriculum pages. GRE optional≠waived; FAQ says scores not reviewed.
