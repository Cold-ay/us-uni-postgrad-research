## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: not_accepted — CSE How to Apply / FAQs: GRE scores are neither required nor considered / not expected; committee will not consider any submitted scores.
- GMAT: not_required — Not used for CSE Rackham MS/MSE admissions.
- TOEFL: required — min 84 — Non-native English speakers must demonstrate proficiency unless Rackham exemption met. Michigan Engineering Academic Services minima (College page CSE applicants are directed to follow): TOEFL iBT overall 84 before 2026-01-21 and 4.5 on/after 2026-01-21; ETS code 1839. CSE apply page points to Rackham exemptions.
- IELTS: required — min 6.5 — Academic IELTS minimum overall 6.5 (Michigan Engineering Academic Services English proficiency table). Official electronic scores to University of Michigan–Ann Arbor.
- English waiver: Rackham exemptions include: native English speaker; Puerto Rico applicant; completed all undergraduate education and earned undergraduate degree at English-only instruction institution; completed all graduate education and earned graduate degree at English-only instruction institution; current U-M student. One-year research-only master's typically does not qualify.
- GPA: recommended — minimum 3.5 — scale 4.0 — CSE Graduate Admissions: successful applicants usually have undergraduate GPA of at least 3.5/4.0 (not a hard requirement).
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 3 — Three letters via Rackham online recommendation system only (no email/mail/Interfolio). Suggested ≥2 from academia.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as statement of purpose; personal statement — Academic Statement of Purpose (≤3 pages) on academic/research background, career goals, and fit with CSE. Separate Personal Statement (≤500 words) also required—not identical to SOP.
- Resume: required — Resume highlighting education, work, research publications, teaching, professional activities, volunteer service, honors/awards.
- Transcripts: required — Upload electronic official transcripts for each bachelor’s/master’s/professional/doctoral degree earned or in progress; official to Rackham if recommended/admitted. Non-U.S.: follow Rackham country credentials guidance.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not listed on CSE How to Apply checklist or admissions FAQs reviewed.
- Fee: $90 — waivers: Rackham fee waivers for U.S. citizens/permanent residents with demonstrated financial need (Pell and/or SAI <$1,500); request before submission. Individual programs may also offer waivers. — Rackham: $75 U.S. citizens/permanent residents; $90 non-U.S. citizens; $10 current Rackham students. amountUsd records international rate commonly used for intl CS applicants.
- Other: Master's (MS/MSE) deadline January 15 (Fall only); PhD Dec 15; SUGS separate.
- Other: MS vs MSE: same requirements; MSE only if bachelor's in engineering.
- Other: Admission to Master's typically without departmental financial aid.
- Other: Cannot hold same CS MS/PhD already earned elsewhere and re-apply for same degree at Michigan.

GRE is not_accepted (not considered), not merely optional. Include both SOP and Personal Statement.
