## Tuition and application timing

$38,233 — Academic-year tuition and mandatory fees (Academic Master, nonresident, continuing); 2026–27.

2026–27 reference, not a Fall 2027 quote or full-degree total.

UCLA Registrar public fee tables Academic Master annual 2026–27 nonresident continuing total $38,232.76 (Tuition $13,722 + NRST $15,102 + Student Services Fee $1,344 + UCSHIP $7,516.98 + campus fees). California resident continuing total without NRST $23,130.76. Grad Division rounds these to about $38,233 / $23,131. New students add one-time Document Fee $97.43. CS MS uses the standard Academic Master schedule (not a self-supporting program). NRST PhD candidacy waiver does not apply to MS.

Application timing: Apps mid-Sep · Deadline 15 Dec 2026 · 23:59 Pacific (MS/PhD Fall only — Grad Division 2027–28). Confirm deadline time zone and personal requirements on the official page.

## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: not_required — UCLA Grad Division 2026–2027 Computer Science admission requirements table: GRE: Not required. CS department page still carries Fall 2025–2026 optional-language for that prior cycle; for 2026–2027 cycle prefer Grad Division “Not required.” GMAT may not be substituted. Subject GRE not reviewed.
- GMAT: not_accepted — CS Graduate Application Requirements: GMAT may not be substituted for the GRE.
- TOEFL: required — min 87 — Intl / non-exempt: TOEFL iBT ≥87 (legacy) or overall ≥4.5 new scale (tests on/after Jan 21, 2026) per Grad English Requirements / CS page. UCLA code 4837; CS dept code 78. Valid within 2 years of application submit. Duolingo not accepted. FAQ also cites legacy subscores W25/S24/R21/L17.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS Academic overall ≥7.0. Send to major department and Division of Graduate Education.
- English waiver: Exempt if bachelor’s or higher from accredited U.S. university or from country where English is sole language of instruction per WHED; or completed two years full-time study at such institutions. Citizenship alone does not exempt.
- GPA: required — minimum 3 — scale 4.0 — University cumulative GPA ≥3.0 for consideration. Dept reviews ≥3.0; most competitive ≥3.5 undergrad and ≥3.50 any graduate study; average admitted ~3.60.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 3 — At least three letters via online application by Dec 15. MS: prefer professors from junior/senior years. (ESAP MS has special 2-letter CS-faculty rule.)
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Statement of Purpose (+ Personal Statement) — SOP 500-word limit in online application (prompts on Grad Division site). Also required Personal Statement 500-word limit. SOP serves as writing sample.
- Resume: optional — Resume/vitae not required; awards/distinctions/publications may be entered in online application sections instead.
- Transcripts: required — Upload one official transcript per post-secondary institution in online application by Dec 15. Final official docs after offer. China/India specific degree-certificate rules on CS page. UCLA students: MyUCLA record OK.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not listed among CS graduate application requirements.
- Fee: $155 — waivers: Need-based and special-program waivers for eligible U.S. citizens/PR (and listed programs) per Grad Admissions FAQs; upload docs in Fee Waiver section. No exceptions beyond stated criteria. — UCLA Grad Admissions FAQ: $135 U.S. citizens/PR; $155 all other applicants. amountUsd = international rate.
- Other: Fall only; deadline Dec 15 23:59 Pacific. Apply mid-Sep–Dec 15.
- Other: Apply to MS or PhD—not both. Applicants who already hold an MS in CS or closely related field not considered for MS.
- Other: MS funding: not eligible for departmental support; TA/GSR/reader not guaranteed; intl MS must show ≥~$65,000 first-year support for visa if admitted.
- Other: Program-requirements Grad page is degree requirements (thesis/capstone), not admissions checklist—use CS graduate-requirements.
