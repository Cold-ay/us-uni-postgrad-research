## Academic direction

Siebel School offers a 32-credit professional MCS (coursework-only; Urbana typically ≤3 consecutive semesters) and a separate 32-credit research MS (thesis via CS 599). Eight optional graduate tracks (AI, data science, security, etc.).

## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — Siebel School does not require GRE for any graduate programs. Scores recommended but not required; applicants may scan official score report PDF to application (official ETS report not required). MCS overview: no GRE required for admission.
- GMAT: not_required — Not part of Siebel CS/MCS admissions; GRE is the only standardized test discussed.
- TOEFL: required — min 5 — Illinois Graduate College English proficiency for admission (unless exempt). Full-status TOEFL iBT minimum 5.0 for scores dated after 2026-01-20 (103 prior to 2026-01-21); limited-status 4.0 (79 prior). Institution code 1836. Siebel also notes funding/TA speak minima separately.
- IELTS: required — min 7.5 — IELTS Academic full-status minimum 7.5; limited-status 6.5 (Graduate College). Send electronically to University of Illinois Urbana Champaign Graduate.
- English waiver: Exempt if post-secondary degree from U.S. or approved English-primary country within five years of proposed term; or ≥2 years post-secondary study or professional work in U.S./approved country within five years. Medium of instruction alone (if country not on exempt list) does not waive. Duolingo accepted for admission (115 limited / 135 full) but not for TA speak requirement.
- GPA: required — minimum 3 — scale 4.0 — Campus Graduate College: at least 3.0/4.0 in last two years of undergraduate study required for eligibility. MCS app-info recommends undergraduate GPA 3.2/4.0 or higher (recommended, not a hard MCS-only floor beyond campus rule).
- Letters of recommendation: optional — MCS (Urbana/Chicago/Online): letters not required; will be considered if included (especially to justify experience in lieu of coursework). Research MS with thesis / PhD / MS Bioinformatics / BS-MS: three letters required. Checklist marks MCS LOR as None.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Academic Statement of Purpose; Personal Statement — Academic Statement of Purpose required (generally 1–2 pages: goals, background, work history, interests). Personal Statement also required for CS admissions (≤250 words).
- Resume: required — CV/resume required as part of online application (education, jobs, research for MS/PhD, achievements, publications, awards, leadership).
- Transcripts: required — Upload scanned unofficial transcripts (with grading scale) for each post-secondary institution; official credentials only if recommended for admission / after admit.
- Interview: unknown — No interview requirement stated on MCS overview, MCS app-info, or Applications Process & Requirements pages reviewed.
- Fee: $90 — waivers: May qualify per Graduate College Application Fee Waivers; contact grad-admissions@siebelschool.illinois.edu with questions. — Effective Spring 2026 cycle: $90 for all applicants (domestic and international), non-refundable, credit card with web application.
- Other: Tracks: coursework MCS (campus Urbana, Chicago, Online) vs research MS with thesis—application materials differ (esp. LORs).
- Other: MCS prereqs for non-CS bachelor's: OOP, data structures, algorithms, linear algebra, statistics/probability (or commensurate experience).
- Other: Applicants already holding an equivalent CS/CE master's are not eligible for MS or MCS.
- Other: Urbana/Chicago MCS Fall deadline May 1; Spring Oct 15 (CS MS Fall Dec 1)—confirm cycle on deadlines page.
- Other: Personal Statement (≤250 words) is additional required essay beyond Academic SOP.

Given URL is professional MCS; research MS has stricter LOR (3). Report both under illinois-cs; site should distinguish MCS vs MS thesis checklists.
