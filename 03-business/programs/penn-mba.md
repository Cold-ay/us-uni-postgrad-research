## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — All applicants must submit GMAT (classic or Focus) or GRE; test center or at-home accepted. Self-report OK; official scores if admitted. Valid within 5-year window by round (e.g. R1 Sep 8 2021–Sep 8 2026). Code GRE 6802. AWA not currently required. No minimum posted.
- GMAT: required — GMAT or GMAT Focus accepted as alternative to GRE. Code G56-97-73. Same validity windows. No minimum posted.
- TOEFL: required — TOEFL iBT required if degree not from English-instruction institution. Code D810-99. English tests valid ~2 years by round window. No minimum score posted (Class of 2022 average TOEFL was 115 for those who took it—historical, not a cutoff).
- IELTS: required — IELTS accepted along with TOEFL iBT, PTE, and Duolingo English Test. No minimum posted on Application Guide.
- English waiver: Waived if you earned a degree from an institution where English was the language of instruction (Application Guide). FAQ also: undergrad or Master’s in English-speaking country or English-instruction institution—include waiver letter + documentation in application. PTE and DET also accepted.
- GPA: not_stated — No numeric minimum GPA on Application Guide; non-4.0 scales should not be converted—report as on transcript.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One letter from someone well acquainted with work performance, preferably current/former supervisor. GMAC Common LOR. Carey JD/MBA still requires two.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Essays — Essay 1 short-forms: immediate post-MBA goal (50 words) + medium/long-term goals (150 words). Essay 2: how you will add meaningful value to Wharton community (350 words). Reapplicant essay 250 words if applicable. Additional Information optional (500 words).
- Resume: required — Current one-page résumé as PDF (≤10 MB); highlight skills, experience, leadership, growth. No password/macros.
- Transcripts: required — Upload transcripts from all undergrad/grad programs (unofficial or self-report template OK). Official required if admitted. Non-English records need authorized English translation.
- Interview: by_invitation — Interviews by invitation only after application review. No candidate admitted without an interview. Format: Team-Based Discussion (virtual) plus short one-on-one (Interview Process page).
- Fee: $275 — waivers: U.S. military active duty or veteran (follow in-app instructions). Moelis Advance Access fee is $100 with separate hardship/military waiver path. — Non-refundable $275 payable by credit card with application (Visa/MC/AmEx).
- Other: 2026–27 rounds: R1 Sep 8 2026; R2 Jan 5 2027; R3 Mar 31 2027; Deferred Apr 21 2027 (5:00 p.m. ET except Deferred 11:59 p.m. ET)
- Other: Every application gets two independent reads
- Other: Bachelor’s from accredited U.S. college or foreign equivalent required

Apply R1/R2 if needing a student visa or as reapplicant. No general GMAT/GRE waiver—plan to test. Prepare for TBD interview format if invited.
