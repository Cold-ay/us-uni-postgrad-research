## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — Test score not required. Applicants may submit GRE, GMAT, GMAT Focus, or EA, or opt out and instead submit a brief MBA-readiness essay showing quantitative/analytical prep (coursework, certifications, professional experience, expired scores). No minimum if submitting; highest total sitting used (no superscore). English proficiency optionality does NOT apply. GRE code 0771.
- GMAT: optional — GMAT/GMAT Focus/EA optional under same flexible policy as GRE. Code 2NZ-2F-24. UCLA Law/Medicine concurrent students may use LSAT/MCAT respectively.
- TOEFL: required — min 87 — UCLA Grad admission minimum TOEFL iBT 87 (before Jan 21 2026) or 4.5 (on/after Jan 21 2026). Anderson FAQ: almost all admits score over 100 iBT. Code 4837-02 for Anderson MBA. Valid within 2 years of application submission; not later than round deadline. Work experience in English does not waive.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS Academic overall 7.0 minimum (UCLA Grad). Anderson FAQ notes almost all admits ≥7.0. Send to Uni of California, Los Angeles-Anderson School of Management.
- English waiver: Exempt with bachelor’s or higher from accredited U.S. university or from a country/institution where English is the sole language of instruction per WHED. Citizenship, U.S. residence, and work experience are not substitutes. Anderson emphasizes education in English—not work—as the key factor.
- GPA: not_stated — Four-year bachelor’s or equivalent required to enroll; three-year undergrad may be admitted if profile strong. No numeric minimum GPA; do not convert non-4.0 scales.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two recommendations for new applicants (one new for reapplicants), preferably direct supervisor/manager in work setting. Online form with standard prompts.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Short essay prompts — Three short essays for 2026–2027: (1) short-/long-term goals + Anderson resources (250 words); (2) how classmates will describe you / values as leader (250); (3) first thing you’d tell classmates (50). Optional extenuating-circumstances essay (250). Test-optional applicants also submit MBA-readiness essay.
- Resume: required — Résumé must be submitted; ideally one page, max two. Does not replace Employment section of application.
- Transcripts: required — Scanned academic records with application; official hard copies only if invited to interview (upon request). International: multiple docs per International Students page + translations.
- Interview: by_invitation — By invitation only after review; an interview must be completed to be admitted. Zoom or in person (~30 min), usually by trained second-year students; blind except résumé. All candidates being considered for admission are interviewed.
- Fee: $200 — waivers: U.S. military applicants; current UCLA graduate students; SIEML program participants—upload docs in application; do not pay first (no refunds). — $200 online application fee. Applications for Class of 2029 noted as opening Aug 1, 2025 on requirements page.
- Other: Rounds: Oct 1 2026 / Jan 6 2027 / Apr 7 2027 (decisions Dec 11 2026 / Mar 26 2027 / May 14 2027)
- Other: Early career and college seniors may apply; most students have work experience
- Other: Apply to only one Anderson program per year

Test-optional ≠ English-optional. If skipping GMAT/GRE, invest in the readiness essay with quant evidence. Plan for interview if competitive—required to receive an offer.
