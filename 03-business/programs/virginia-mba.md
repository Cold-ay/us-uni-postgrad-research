## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — Verified GRE accepted equally with GMAT (prior/current), EA, MCAT, LSAT unless approved test waiver. ETS code 4629. Report all valid scores; cancelled/expired noted in counts only. Verified score within 1 week of round deadline for completeness. No minimum. Waiver case-by-case (strong GPA vs class avg ~3.5, analytical degree/coursework, ≥3 years quant/analytical work, optional SAT/ACT/CPA/CFA).
- GMAT: required — GMAT Previous/Current Editions accepted; GMAC code KC7-0X-86. Superscores accepted and all valid attempts reviewed. EA/MCAT/LSAT also accepted equivalently. Waiver path as in GRE detail—waiver ≠ optional for all.
- TOEFL: required — Required if bachelor's medium of instruction was not English. TOEFL code 5820. No minimum requirement; average TOEFL cited as 106. Valid 2 years. Unofficial scores needed before submit; official to Darden.
- IELTS: required — IELTS accepted (search University of Virginia - Darden School of Business). PTE and Duolingo English Test also accepted (DET: UVA Darden). No published minimum.
- English waiver: Not required if bachelor's degree from university where medium of instruction was English. Master's/PhD with English MOI (if bachelor's was not) — English test strongly recommended.
- GPA: not_stated — No minimum GPA. Holistic review across academics, professional experience, personal qualities.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One recommendation required; optional second if new insights. Prefer recent professional supervisor. Electronic ratings grid + short answers (interaction; peer comparison; constructive feedback given).
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Short answer questions; video essay — 2026–27 cycle: two short answers (200 words each)—(1) Relationships Matter Here: what classmates should know not on resume; (2) Careers With Purpose: short-term post-MBA goal (industry/function/geography/size/mission) and long-term alignment—plus one 90-second video essay (random Admissions Committee prompt).
- Resume: required — Typically one-page if <10 years experience. Separate employment history section in application; quantify impact; personal interests welcome.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial transcripts for all degree-related coursework uploaded. Non-English: full literal translation with official transcript. Official transcripts from degree-granting institutions before start if matriculating. Three-year bachelor's degrees recognized if equivalent.
- Interview: by_invitation — Interview required before acceptance; by invitation after review (Admissions Committee or Second Years). Early Action offers limited open interview slots applicants can schedule before submitting.
- Fee: $250 — waivers: Event reductions: $100 off (to $150) for partner/off-site Darden events or qualifying webinars; $150 off (to $100) for in-person Charlottesville or Darden DC Metro events (May 1 2026–May 1 2027 attendance). Full waivers: 2025–26 reapplicants; U.S. military veterans/active duty; current/recently separated government employees; National Service alumni (AmeriCorps/Peace Corps/TFA/Teach for All); first-generation college students; UVA alumni/current UVA grad students; alumni of Virginia colleges. Reductions not stackable. — Nonrefundable $250 application fee.
- Other: Class of 2029 / Aug 2027 matriculation. Deadlines: EA Sep 9 2026; R1 Oct 7 2026; R2 Jan 7 2027; R3 Mar/Apr/May 1 2027.
- Other: Future Year Scholars deferred pathway for college seniors (separate 2026 deadlines).
- Other: Does not admit applicants who already hold an AACSB-accredited MBA.
- Other: Accounting and statistics recommended before matriculation (not prerequisites).

Verified test or approved waiver required—optional ≠ waived. Apply test waiver 3–4 weeks before target round. International applicants strongly encouraged Early Action/R1; Round 3 by Apr 1 for visa timing.
