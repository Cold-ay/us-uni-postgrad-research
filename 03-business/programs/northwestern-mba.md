## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — How-to-apply: submit GMAT or GRE from a single sitting (no superscores); no preference; no minimum. Scores valid 5 years; complete application before score expiration. GRE code 7921. (Evening & Weekend MBA has different optional/waiver rules—this item is Full-Time.)
- GMAT: required — GMAT code 6WZ-3J-89. Single sitting only; same validity/no-minimum policy as GRE.
- TOEFL: required — Non-native English speakers must show proficiency. If exemption criteria not met, evaluated via application/interview plus valid TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo. No numeric minimums posted on FT international-candidates page. Committee may require a score from any candidate.
- IELTS: required — IELTS accepted with TOEFL/DET when a score is needed; no posted minimum on FT international page.
- English waiver: Satisfied without test if: undergrad/grad degree from U.S. college/university; OR undergrad/grad degree where English was only language of instruction (provide URL/docs); OR worked in U.S. or English-speaking nation ≥2 consecutive years within last 5 years.
- GPA: not_stated — Bachelor’s or equivalent by matriculation required. No numeric minimum GPA on how-to-apply. Encourage pre-enrollment intro calculus and statistics.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two LORs; ideally one current supervisor/manager and one other professional evaluator of performance/leadership. Additional letters neither required nor encouraged. Prompts: navigating differing perspectives (300 words); performance vs peers (300); most important constructive feedback (250).
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Written essay / video essays — One written essay for all programs (550 words max; Part I career path/why now; Part II contribution to classmates). Five video essay questions (~60 seconds each; video essays due 96 hours after application deadline; link on status page after submit+payment). Reapplicants: growth essay 250 words.
- Resume: required — Current resume/CV plus detailed employment history section. Strongly recommend ≥2 years full-time work upon matriculation (except Kellogg Future Leaders deferred).
- Transcripts: required — List all colleges/universities/study abroad; upload transcripts. Official hard copies only upon matriculation. Non-English records need original + authorized verbatim English translation. Conferred date or diploma required before matriculation.
- Interview: by_invitation — All interviews virtual. Invitations emailed on rolling basis through evaluation period until decision release; timing not indicative of status. Scheduled interview time cannot be changed; cancel via Admissions if needed.
- Fee: $250 — waivers: Active duty U.S. military or U.S. veterans (pay stub/command letter or DD-214). Candidates employed by Teach For America, Title 1 schools, AmeriCorps, or Peace Corps within past three years (supporting evidence). Non-refundable. — $250 via credit card in application; do not send payment directly to Kellogg.
- Other: 2026–27 FT MBA rounds: R1 Sep 9 2026; R2 Jan 6 2027; R3 Mar 31 2027.
- Other: One full-time Kellogg program application per year; may select one alternate FT program and/or Evening & Weekend with short interest paragraph.
- Other: All Kellogg Full-Time MBA programs STEM-designated.
- Other: Internationals encouraged to apply R1 or R2 for visa timing; application name must match passport for I-20.

Use FT how-to-apply + international-candidates pages (not EW optional-test rules). Video essays are after deadline (+96h)—plan tech setup. GMAT/GRE required for Full-Time per how-to-apply.
