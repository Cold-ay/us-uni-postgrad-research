## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — Valid standardized test required for Full-time MBA (Fall 2027 cycle). GRE accepted with no preference vs GMAT/EA/LSAT/MCAT/DAT. No minimum score; highest overall sitting used (no GMAT superscore). Codes: GRE 4455. Case-by-case standardized test waiver by request (deadline Apr 1, 2027); NYU undergrad alumni with final GPA ≥3.20 get automatic waiver (NYU + MBA). Andre Koo Tech MBA is separately test-optional for 2027—not this Full-time MBA route.
- GMAT: required — GMAT or GMAT Focus (or EA/LSAT/MCAT/DAT) accepted as the required standardized test for Full-time MBA. Code 5TF-HW-35. Same waiver paths as GRE. No minimum; highest overall sitting.
- TOEFL: required — TOEFL required unless native English speaker or undergrad/grad degree taught in English. No minimum score to apply; highest score considered (MyBest not used). Valid 2 years at submission. Code 2582. DET also accepted.
- IELTS: required — IELTS accepted as English proficiency alternative; same waiver rules as TOEFL. No minimum posted. Report to New York University - MBA and Graduate Admissions.
- English waiver: Not required if native language is English, or undergraduate/graduate degree was taught in English. Students who completed a university degree in India are not required to submit TOEFL/IELTS/DET. DET also accepted (report NYU Stern, MBA & Graduate Admissions).
- GPA: not_stated — Stern states no minimum cumulative GPA; evaluates varied grading systems holistically (Transcripts & Degrees).
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One EQ Endorsement required, preferably from current supervisor (explain in application if not). Second EQ Endorsement optional. More than two allowed but not encouraged. Immediate family may not write. Online form with EQ-specific question.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Essays / Pick Six — Essay 1 Professional Aspirations (500 words max): short-term career goals and why Stern MBA. Essay 2 Personal Expression / Pick Six (six images + captions PDF). Essay 3 Additional Information optional (500 words).
- Resume: required — Upload résumé covering full-time post-university work and education; internships/part-time/extracurriculars/volunteer may be included (Professional Information).
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial scanned transcripts from all undergrad/grad institutions for application (courses, credits, grades, degree name). Official transcripts + degree conferral required before enrollment if admitted. Four-year bachelor’s or foreign equivalent (three-year OK for listed countries e.g. India).
- Interview: by_invitation — Interviews by invitation only after complete application; conducted virtually by trained admissions professionals. Initial notification may be interview invite, waitlist, or denial. NYU x NYU page: interview required for admission to any full-time MBA and offered by invitation only. May be waitlisted/denied without interview.
- Fee: $250 — waivers: Economic hardship (case-by-case); U.S. military active/veteran (docs); Teach for America/India; Forté MBALaunch/On Demand alumni; NYU undergrad alumni (no form); reapplicants within past two cycles fee not required. Request form before paying (non-refundable once paid). — NYU Stern application fee $250 (stated on NYU x NYU / Stern page as the Stern application fee). Application not processed until fee received.
- Other: Fall 2027 Full-time MBA deadlines: Sep 15 2026 / Oct 15 2026 / Jan 15 2027 / Apr 15 2027 (11:59 p.m. ET)
- Other: Program selection required: Full-time MBA vs Luxury & Retail vs Andre Koo Tech (apply to only one)
- Other: Also accepts EA, LSAT, MCAT, DAT for standardized test requirement
- Other: International applicants encouraged to apply earlier for visa timing

Full-time MBA still requires a test unless waiver approved—do not confuse with 2027 Andre Koo Tech test-optional policy. Submit waiver ≥3 weeks before apply. Pick Six must be a single PDF.
