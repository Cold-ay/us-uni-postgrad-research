## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — GMAT or GRE typically required (scores ≤5 years old at application). Self-report unofficial in Phase 1; official required in Phase 2 if advancing. Best scores considered; seek verbal/quant balance. GRE code IU Bloomington 1324. Candidates may apply with waiver by indicating in Phase 1 (no separate waiver application on admissions requirements page)—committee weighs strong prior academics (esp. quant), CPA/CFA or equivalents. FAQ also describes email waiver request path with resume/transcripts/300-word statement and round-tied deadlines—prefer Phase 1 indication per current admissions requirements page; confirm if portal still uses email path.
- GMAT: required — GMAT accepted (code 8ZG-41-37 Full-Time MBA). Same typical-requirement + Phase 1 waiver indication as GRE. Strong scores help differentiate; some consulting/IB employers still ask for scores.
- TOEFL: required — min 100 — International applicants whose native language is not English: TOEFL iBT min 100 (120 scale) or 5 (6-point scale). Scores ≤2 years old. Code 1324. Required before submitting application.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS band min 7.0, or DET 130. Scores ≤2 years old.
- English waiver: Automatically waived for international applicants who earned undergrad or post-grad degree from an institution where English was the language of instruction; indicate in application or optional essay.
- GPA: not_stated — Transcripts with cumulative GPA from each institution required; no numeric minimum GPA stated on Full-Time MBA admissions requirements page. Baseline expectation: algebra, spreadsheets, statistics familiarity.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One professional recommendation; current or recent supervisor preferred.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Personal essays — Personal essays in Phase 2 (if invited). Admissions requirements emphasize communication skills, unique background, and career goals; exact prompts not fully enumerated on public how-to-apply/requirements pages—confirm in Kelley Application Portal.
- Resume: required — Resume required to start Phase 1 (Kelley Graduate Application Form is free). Most applicants ≥2 years into career.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial during application process; official before enrollment. International: document degree as recognized U.S. bachelor’s equivalent.
- Interview: by_invitation — Two-phase process: Phase 1 (form + resume) free. If invited to Phase 2, portal requirements may include admissions interview; full application reviewed once documentation received and interview complete.
- Fee: $75 — waivers: Phase 1 is free. University application fee paid in Phase 2 if invited. — $75 university application fee in Phase 2 (how-to-apply checklist).
- Other: Fall 2027 start (portal). Rounds with Phase 1 / Phase 2 deadlines: R1 Oct 1 / Oct 15; R2 Dec 15 / Jan 5; R3 Feb 15 / Mar 1; R4 Apr 1 / Apr 15 (international candidates no later than Round 2 recommended).
- Other: +Flex: decide end of Year 1 whether Year 2 on campus or Kelley Direct online (visa implications for internationals).
- Other: Consortium school; KAAP deferred admission for college seniors (U.S. citizens/PR; deadline June 1).

Phase 1 free—resume only to start. Confirm essay prompts and any email-vs-portal waiver mechanics in portal/FAQ. English DET accepted (130).
