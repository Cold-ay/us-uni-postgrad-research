## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — GMAT, GRE, or EA accepted; no minimum. Middle 80% applicants historically GMAT ~660–740 (descriptive). Valid 5 years. Standardized test waiver may be requested in application if STEM/business/quant degree with ≥3.0 GPA (or equiv), CPA/CFA, or analytical full-time work—approval not guaranteed; decision in 5–7 business days. Future Leaders: GRE/GMAT required, no waiver. GRE code 8296.
- GMAT: optional — GMAT/GMAT 10th/EA accepted (Two-Year MBA code 5JW-BM-96). Same waiver policy as GRE. Sage Scholarship requires a test score even if waiver otherwise available.
- TOEFL: required — min 100 — If English not native and no university degree in listed English-speaking country: TOEFL iBT overall ≥100 with ≥25 each section (or CEFR level 5). No MyBest/superscore; no TOEFL Essentials. Institution 2098 dept 02. Valid 2 years.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS Academic overall 7.0 with Speaking and Writing ≥7.0. DET overall 120 also accepted. Electronic to Cornell Two Year MBA Admissions.
- English waiver: Exempt if native citizen of U.S./UK/Ireland/Australia/NZ/South Africa/Ghana/Canada; OR university degree from those countries; OR degree from non-English country with official documentation that sole language of instruction was English. India/Pakistan/Philippines/Hong Kong/Singapore citizenship alone does not exempt. Residency/work experience alone does not waive.
- GPA: not_stated — Bachelor’s required for enrollment; report grades as on transcript (do not convert non-4.0 scales). No published minimum GPA cutoff.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One professional LOR required; up to two allowed. GMAC Common LOR. Supervisor/colleague/client preferred.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Goals statement + admissions essay — Goals statement (short-/long-term role/company/industry + leverage unique qualities) plus choice of one required essay (Impact 350w OR Table of Contents/creative ≤350w or multimedia). Optional essay (required for reapplicants).
- Resume: required — Professional resume on application checklist; also list of activities.
- Transcripts: required — Academic records for all post-secondary coursework/degrees; unofficial OK to apply; official by deposit deadline if enrolling. Certified English translation if needed.
- Interview: by_invitation — After preliminary review: invitation to interview, waitlist without interview, or denial (Important Dates). Final decisions after interview on rolling basis by round final-notification date.
- Fee: $250 — waivers: U.S. military service members/veterans; Forté MBALaunch; Peace Corps; Teach for America alumni; current Cornell students—email coordinator before submit (≥3 business days before deadline). — Nonrefundable $250 USD.
- Other: Two-Year Ithaca MBA requirements—not Johnson Cornell Tech MBA
- Other: Rounds: R1 Sep 17 2026; Consortium early Oct 15 2026; R2/Consortium traditional Jan 5 2027; R3 Apr 6 2027
- Other: Enrollment deposits $1,500 + $1,500; Re Vera background check on matriculation

Test optional only with approved waiver—Sage Scholarship needs a score. Confirm English waiver country list carefully (e.g., India citizenship ≠ auto-exempt).
