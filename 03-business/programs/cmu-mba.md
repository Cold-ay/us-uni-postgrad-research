## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — GMAT, GMAT Focus, GRE, or EA required unless approved test waiver. GRE code Carnegie Mellon Tepper 4883. Scores valid 5 years at submit. Waiver case-by-case for academic readiness; does not signal admission; scholarship eligibility unaffected. Pre-check eligibility form available.
- GMAT: required — GMAT/GMAT Focus Full-Time MBA code 69H-XH-99; EA accepted (strong quant coursework and/or STEM professional experience expected). No superscore as official; holistic review of all submitted scores. Waiver alternative available case-by-case.
- TOEFL: required — TOEFL required unless bachelor’s or graduate degree with sole language of instruction English. Recommended iBT 25 each section; new TOEFL iBT overall ≥5 with section ≥5. Code 2074 Carnegie Mellon Graduate Business. Valid 2 years.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — Recommended IELTS overall 7 and 7 in each band. Select Carnegie Mellon and Tepper. Duolingo DET also accepted (recommended overall 120).
- English waiver: Waived if bachelor’s (or graduate—recommend still testing) from institution where sole language of instruction was English. Non-U.S. degrees: documents must state English sole medium for entire university, or upload official Registrar letter.
- GPA: not_stated — No published minimum GPA. Prerequisite: one semester college-level calculus with letter grade B or better (or complete Tepper Calculus Fundamentals before matriculation).
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One professional recommendation; ideally current/previous supervisor. GMAC Common LOR.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Written essay + video goals essay — One required written essay (max 500 words)—choose 1 of 2 prompts (uncertainty/action or professional/personal transformation). Required video essay on post-MBA career goals (up to 2 minutes). Optional essay for gaps/context/reapplicant strengthening.
- Resume: required — 1–2 page resume; standard 12-pt font; include Month/Year start and end dates for each role.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial scanned university transcripts (not web/portal downloads) from all undergrad/grad; degree name/conferral; grading scale; translations if needed. Official after admit.
- Interview: by_invitation — Evaluative behavioral interview by invitation only and required. Invitations may begin within one week of deadline through decision date.
- Fee: $200 — waivers: May earn waiver by attending a Tepper event; reapplicants within last year waived. — $200 payable online by credit card at submission.
- Other: Deadlines: R1 Sep 30 2026; R2 Jan 8 2027; R3 Mar 3 2027 (final for international FT); R4 May 5 2027; R4 Final Jun 15 2027
- Other: Four-year bachelor’s or equivalent required
- Other: Re Vera verification for admitted students who accept

Plan GMAT/GRE/EA unless you obtain an approved waiver before/at review. Video essay is mandatory. International applicants: Round 3 is last FT round.
