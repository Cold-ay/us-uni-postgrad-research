## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — Self-reported GMAT, GRE, or Executive Assessment (EA); no minimum score. Official scores required only after admission. Select applicants may request test-score waiver case-by-case if meeting stated criteria (e.g., ≥2 years U.S. military or relevant progressive full-time work or current other UGA grad program PLUS strong U.S. academics / advanced degree / quantitative cert / strong quant coursework). Meeting criteria does not guarantee waiver. Strong scores help for consulting/finance recruiting and assistantships/scholarships.
- GMAT: required — GMAT, GRE, or EA accepted; no minimum. Same waiver process as GRE. Official reports after admit.
- TOEFL: required — min 100 — Required if degree from non-U.S. accredited institution in a country where English is not primary/official language. Preferred mins: TOEFL 100 overall (exams before Jan 21 2026) or 5.0 overall (exams on/after Jan 22 2026). Official scores only after admission. International students still need TOEFL/IELTS even if GMAT/GRE waived.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS 7.0 overall band preferred when English proficiency required.
- English waiver: English proficiency required for degrees from non-U.S. institutions in non-English-primary/official-language countries; see FTMBA International Student Applicants page for full list. GMAT/GRE waiver does not waive English tests.
- GPA: required — minimum 3 — scale 4.0 — Baccalaureate (or foreign equivalent) from regionally accredited institution. Candidates expected to have minimum 3.0 GPA; may still apply below 3.0 with optional written statement addressing academic performance.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two letters; professional recommendations preferred.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Short written responses / optional video essay — Three short written responses (250 words max each: career goals; persistence challenge; something classmates wouldn’t learn from résumé) + one optional written response (250 words). Optional video essay (2 minutes max).
- Resume: required — One-page business résumé, reverse chronological; quantify impact; include education/employment dates, titles, FT/PT.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial copies from each institution attended for college credit for application; official transcripts required after admission.
- Interview: by_invitation — By invitation after application review. Virtual available for internationals and domestics unable to travel. Campus Visit Day may include Admissions Committee interview, lunch with students, class visit.
- Fee: $100 — $100 application fee paid online at submission. Fee waiver details not enumerated on main Full-Time MBA admissions requirements list.
- Other: ≥2 years professional post-bachelor work experience before entering program (strongly preferred for 2-Year and MBA-MSBA STEM track).
- Other: Fall 2027 rounds (11:59pm ET): R1 Oct 10 2026; R2 Jan 10 2027 (recommended intl); R3 Mar 10 2027 (final intl; recommended for merit/assistantships); R4 May 10 2027; then rolling.
- Other: Dual-degree applicants use Round 4 as final deadline.

Optional video ≠ required. Test waiver is case-by-case (not automatic). Internationals: English tests still required when criteria apply even if GMAT/GRE waived.
