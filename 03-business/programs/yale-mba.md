## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — Verified GMAT or GRE required; Yale SOM does not waive the test score requirement for any applicant. GRE code 3986. Scores valid 5 years and must be valid on submit date; official report by Post-Submission Materials Deadline. Highest score credited; all reported scores considered. No minimum.
- GMAT: required — GMAT (including online/newer shorter versions) required alternatively to GRE—no preference. Send to Yale School of Management MBA, Full-Time via mba.com. LSAT also accepted for law-school-path candidates (email mba.admissions@yale.edu for instructions). No waivers.
- TOEFL: not_required — TOEFL not required for international MBA applicants. Fluency in English required for admission; assessed in part via video questions.
- IELTS: not_required — IELTS and PTE Academic not required for international MBA applicants.
- English waiver: N/A—English proficiency exams are not required; video questions help assess communication/English skills.
- GPA: not_stated — No minimum GPA stated. Report GPA only if listed on transcript; otherwise leave blank and check 'No GPA reported on transcript.' Class Profile has ranges.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two professional recommendations; ideally one from most recent supervisor. Silver Scholars: one academic + one professional. Letters in English (translation OK).
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Essay; video questions; Behavioral Assessment — Choose one of three essay prompts (500-word limit): biggest commitment; most meaningful community; or most significant challenge. Optional Information section truly optional. After submit + fee: video questions (two recorded prompts, 60 seconds each) and ETS Behavioral Assessment (~25 minutes).
- Resume: required — Resume required; typically one page unless >10 years experience. Focus on accomplishments/impact.
- Transcripts: required — Transcripts from every college/university attended (undergrad, graduate, study abroad, transfer, non-degree for credit). Unofficial OK to apply; official if enroll. Non-English: original + notarized English translation. Three-year international degrees may apply.
- Interview: by_invitation — All admitted applicants will have had an interview. Invitations rolling within round; 30-minute blind interview with 2Y student, recent alum, or Admissions. Waitlisted candidates may interview later rounds.
- Fee: $250 — waivers: Sliding scale: $175 if earning <$50k/year; $125 if <$25k/year (based on reported compensation; verified if enroll). Full waivers: U.S. military/veterans; alumni/students of ~700 economic-diversity U.S. colleges (≥35% Pell); Girls Who Invest alumni; Forté MBALaunch; Peace Corps; current Yale students; MLT MBA Prep Fellows; Teach for America/China/India/Teach for All; QuestBridge seniors/alumni. Request ≥48 hours before deadline. — Standard fee $250 with income-based sliding scale.
- Other: 2026–27 deadlines: R1 Sep 15 2026; R2 Jan 6 2027; R3 Apr 7 2027.
- Other: Consortium and QuestBridge Graduate School Match pathways available.
- Other: Silver Scholars Program for college seniors (same application with recommender differences).
- Other: Full-time work experience strongly encouraged but not required (avg ~5 years).

GMAT/GRE required with no waivers—do not treat as optional. No TOEFL/IELTS needed. Complete video questions and Behavioral Assessment promptly after fee payment.
