## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — GRE accepted equally with GMAT Focus. Program is not test-optional. GRE school code R6897. Upload unofficial score; official required if admitted. Automatic waiver path for PhD/PharmD/DBA or U.S.-based MD/JD or MCAT/LSAT report; other waivers evaluated on Academic Readiness, Quantitative/Analytic Strength, Leadership/Professional Maturity, Supplemental Evidence (not guaranteed). Scholarship eligibility generally requires a submitted GMAT/GRE.
- GMAT: required — GMAT Focus or GRE required (not test-optional). No strict minimum; FT MBA Class of 2023 average GMAT ~689 (650–700 mid range cited on admissions FAQ). GMAT codes via Jindal GMAC listing. Waiver paths same as GRE detail.
- TOEFL: required — min 80 — International applicants must provide English proficiency. UT Dallas Graduate Admissions: TOEFL iBT min 80 (tests on/before 2026-01-26) or 4 (after 2026-01-26); PBT 550. School code 6897. Scores <2 years old at admission.
- IELTS: required — min 6.5 — IELTS Academic minimum 6.5 (UT Dallas Graduate Admissions). DET min 105 and PTE Academic min 67 also accepted university-wide.
- English waiver: Automatically waived if bachelor's/master's earned through instruction and examination in English; or native country primary language is English (UTD recognized list). MBA FAQ also cites U.S. bachelor's/master's and English-instruction degree.
- GPA: required — minimum 3 — scale 4.0 — UT Dallas Graduate Admissions expects overall GPA of 3.0 (4.0 scale) or better for graduate admission; MBA review is holistic.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One letter from a professional contact (need not be an employer). Submit via online LOR system or PDF to mba@utdallas.edu.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Statement of purpose; second essay — Statement of purpose plus a second essay (each 300–500 words) via applicant portal. Second essay prompts strategies for continued personal/professional success and 5/10/15-year vision after the FT MBA and how the program helps.
- Resume: required — Detailed résumé of all work history required. Two years post-baccalaureate work experience required; exceptions considered for internship experience or strong academic record.
- Transcripts: required — Upload official digital copies of transcripts from all universities attended (unofficial copies not accepted for review upload). If admitted, official transcripts sent directly from issuing university.
- Interview: by_invitation — Interviews by invitation only for Full-Time MBA candidates.
- Fee: $75 — waivers: Automatically waived for UT Dallas alumni, active-duty military, and veterans. — $75 for domestic and international candidates.
- Other: Fall-only Full-Time MBA cohort (18-month). Incomplete files not reviewed.
- Other: Rounds (month-day on table; years unlabeled on FT MBA page): Oct 15 / Jan 8 / Mar 1 / May 1 then rolling.
- Other: MS/MBA dual path: apply to MS per MS rules; combining FT MBA cohort with MS occurs after MBA admission.
- Other: Seat fee $750 within 10 days of admit (programming fee, not tuition deposit).

GMAT/GRE required unless waiver granted—optional ≠ waived. Scholarship competition typically needs a test score. Use Grad Admissions English minima alongside MBA checklist.
