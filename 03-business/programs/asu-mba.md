## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — GMAT or GRE required; send official scores (GRE institution 4007; GMAT 0GZ-1R-80). ASU does not accept GRE General Test at-home. Case-by-case waiver: request via optional essay in application with justification (W. P. Carey Full-time MBA How to apply).
- GMAT: required — GMAT or GRE required (institutional code 0GZ-1R-80). Waiver eligibility via optional essay request; not an automatic/program-wide waiver.
- TOEFL: required — Required if native language is not English. W. P. Carey states graduate minimums may be higher than ASU general; exact Full-time MBA numeric mins not printed on program page. ASU Grad general (exams before Jan 21 2026): iBT 80; on/after Jan 21 2026: total 4. ASU does not accept TOEFL iBT Home/Paper Edition or Essentials for this WPC MBA page note (Home Edition accepted university-wide only for exams on/after Jan 21 2026 per Admission Services). Confirm current WPC MBA threshold with admissions.
- IELTS: required — Accepted as proof of English proficiency when required. ASU Grad general Academic overall ≥6.5; W. P. Carey may set higher—exact Full-time MBA IELTS min not printed on program How-to-apply page.
- English waiver: ASU English proficiency exemptions apply (citizenship on ASU English-speaking country list; U.S. degree; medium-of-instruction documentation; other form-based exemptions on Admission Services English proficiency page). WPC Full-time MBA: international applicants follow same process as domestic plus English proof if required; after admission, visa/financial guarantee steps.
- GPA: required — minimum 3 — scale 4 — ASU Degree Search (BAFULLMBA): minimum cumulative GPA 3.00 (4.00 scale) in last 60 hours of first bachelor’s, or 3.00 in an applicable master’s.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Degree Search requires two letters commenting on motivation, commitment, achievements, work experience, and success potential. Online application collects recommender contact (name, affiliation, work email).
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Admission essay / short-answer questions — Admission essay / short essay responses in graduate application (personal statements not accepted). Degree Search: responses to short-answer questions.
- Resume: required — Current resume with positions held and Month/Year employment dates.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial acceptable at application; if admitted, official transcripts from every college/university awarding bachelor’s or higher. ASU alumni need not resubmit ASU transcripts. International: original language + official English translation.
- Interview: by_invitation — ASU Degree Search: personal interview arranged by invitation only must be completed before an offer of admission.
- Fee: $70 — waivers: Military and Citizen Service fee waiver for U.S. Military, Teach for America, Peace Corps, or AmeriCorps—email wpcareymasters@asu.edu before submitting. — $70 domestic; $115 international (non-refundable). Applications not processed until fee received.
- Other: Fall 2027 rounds: R1 Oct 15 2026; R2 Dec 1 2026; R3 (Priority International) Feb 1 2027; R4 (Final) Apr 1 2027; rolling until Jul 15 2027
- Other: STEM-designated Full-time MBA; August start; ~21 months
- Other: Enrollment deposit $1,000 + matriculation fee $500 after admission

Treat GMAT/GRE as required unless you document a strong waiver case in the optional essay. Confirm W. P. Carey–specific English score floors with admissions—program page says they may exceed ASU Grad mins but does not print the number. Interview is invitation-only before any offer.
