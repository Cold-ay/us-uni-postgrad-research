## Tuition and application timing

$83,946 — Academic-year tuition (Full-Time MBA, non-Michigan resident / international); 2026–27.

2026–27 reference, not a Fall 2027 quote or full-degree total.

Michigan Ross Full-Time MBA Tuition & Financial Aid: 2026–2027 rates approved by the Board of Regents in June 2026 — Non-Michigan Residents/International $83,946 per academic year; Michigan Residents $78,586. Mandatory fees $246.89 per full term; International Student Services Fee $500 per term for F/J visa students. Living-cost guidance (books, food & housing, personal, loan fees) is separate. 2027–2028 rates to be posted July 2027. Full-time MBA only — not Weekend/Online/EMBA.

Application timing: Round 2 · 4 Jan 2027 · 11:59 p.m. ET (Full-Time MBA; R1 passed). Confirm deadline time zone and personal requirements on the official page.

## Internships

MAP is a required seven-week consulting project preparing students for internships and careers. A project is not a guaranteed paid internship.

## Funding

Automatic merit scholarship consideration on admission; $10,000 to full tuition. No separate application. Federal loans US-only; internationals use private loans. True Grit and several awards open to domestic and international students per affording page.

Shared scholarship IDs: SCH-006, SCH-007, SCH-015, SCH-017, SCH-019, SCH-020, SCH-034, SCH-035, SCH-036, SCH-037, SCH-038, SCH-062, SCH-064, SCH-072, SCH-074, SCH-077, SCH-084. See the [scholarship library](../../04-scholarships/README.md).
