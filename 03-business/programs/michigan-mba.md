## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — May submit GMAT, GRE, or Executive Assessment, OR (not both) a Statement of Quantitative Academic Readiness—no separate pre-approval. Self-report or unofficial OK; official if accepted. Highest total sitting used (no mix-and-match). Scores must be unexpired at application. MCAT/LSAT/PCAT/DAT/OAT may be cited inside the Statement alternative.
- GMAT: optional — GMAT accepted as Option 1 standardized test; same either-test-or-Statement rule as GRE.
- TOEFL: required — If native language is not English, demonstrate proficiency via TOEFL iBT (code 1839), PTE, IELTS, or Duolingo. Scores must be active/unexpired at enrollment. No numeric minimums posted on Full-Time MBA application-requirements page.
- IELTS: required — IELTS accepted as TOEFL alternative; send to University of Michigan–Ann Arbor. No numeric minimum on Ross FT MBA requirements page.
- English waiver: Exempt if undergraduate degree from accredited institution where primary language of instruction was English. Alternative: essay demonstrating immersive academic/professional English use (e.g., ≥1 year job with English as primary communication within past 3 years; or English-instruction academic transcripts).
- GPA: not_stated — U.S. bachelor’s equivalent required by application time. Transcripts should include graduation GPA; no minimum GPA stated on checklist.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One recommendation via GMAC Common Recommendation Form. Current/former supervisor ideal; else client, project manager, or professional mentor.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Admissions essays — Part 1 Career Aspirations (300 words): short-term goal + how Ross MBA/program offerings help in first post-MBA role. Part 2 Impact and Growth (200 words): choose 1 of 3 prompts. Optional essay (250 words) for outliers (gaps, academic outliers, recommender choice, etc.) per Class of 2029 admissions blog; checklist page emphasizes Parts 1–2.
- Resume: required — Current education and professional experience with months/years matching application; community service and hobbies/interests encouraged; strongly suggested ≤1 page.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial undergrad/grad transcripts (+ degree docs) with application; official if accepted. Internationals: diploma/degree date docs; certified translations if not English; WES authentication if admitted.
- Interview: by_invitation — By invitation only; conducted by Ross alum or current MBA student. Timeline shared a few weeks after deadline.
- Fee: $250 — waivers: Waived for: U.S. military active/veterans; Peace Corps/AmeriCorps/Teach for America/China/India/Teach for All partners who served; current U-M graduate students; UN least-developed-country citizens (verified); current Fulbright Scholars for U.S. grad school; Ross FT MBA reapplicants within past 5 years; applicants who register and attend ≥1 Michigan Ross FT MBA event before applying. No other waiver reasons. — Non-refundable $250.
- Other: Class of 2029 / Fall 2027: R1 Sep 8 2026; R2 Jan 4 2027; R3 Mar 29 2027 (11:59pm ET).
- Other: Consortium path: R1 Oct 15 / R2 Jan 5 (Consortium deadlines); no separate Ross app if applying via Consortium (still answer Ross essay questions plus Consortium essays).
- Other: Ross Deferred MBA pathway for graduating students.
- Other: Statement of Quantitative Academic Readiness is a fulfillment option, not a ‘waived’ badge—optional ≠ waived.

Choose either test scores or Quantitative Readiness Statement—not both. English mins not posted—plan competitive scores. Event attendance can waive $250 fee.

## Internships

MAP is a required seven-week consulting project preparing students for internships and careers. A project is not a guaranteed paid internship.

## Funding

Automatic merit scholarship consideration on admission; $10,000 to full tuition. No separate application. Federal loans US-only; internationals use private loans. True Grit and several awards open to domestic and international students per affording page.

Shared scholarship IDs: SCH-006, SCH-007, SCH-015, SCH-017, SCH-019, SCH-020, SCH-034, SCH-035, SCH-036, SCH-037, SCH-038, SCH-062, SCH-064, SCH-072, SCH-074, SCH-077, SCH-084. See the [scholarship library](../../04-scholarships/README.md).
