## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — Must self-report GMAT or GRE (all versions accepted; no superscore; no minimum). Valid at submit; GRE code 4887. Case-by-case GMAT/GRE waiver if ≥2 years post-grad work, native English OR bachelor’s sole English instruction, request before deadlines (R1/Consortium1 Aug 12 2026; R2/C2 Nov 9 2026; R3 Mar 5 2027)—not automatic; miss deadline → must submit score that round.
- GMAT: required — GMAT accepted (code Z04-NH-64). Same waiver request process/deadlines as GRE. Official scores for enrolling students; release early if score expires before Aug 1 2027.
- TOEFL: required — Non-native English speakers must submit DET, IELTS Academic, PTE Academic, or TOEFL iBT unless bachelor’s from institution where English is sole language of instruction. No published minimum scores. TOEFL code D507. Online/at-home accepted. DET must be certified to Tuck before submit.
- IELTS: required — IELTS Academic accepted; provide TRF number. No published minimum band on Apply page.
- English waiver: Non-native speakers qualify if bachelor’s degree from institution where English is sole language of instruction—self-indicate in Degrees section; no separate approval documents required for that waiver.
- GPA: not_stated — Report undergrad GPA and additional schools in Degrees section; committee calibrates across grading schemes—no published minimum.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two LORs (one new LOR if reapplicant from most recent cycle). Prefer current direct supervisor; explain if not. GMAC Common LOR questions. Professors/friends/family/coaches discouraged.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Application essays — Three required essays (2000 characters each): goals/growth/why Tuck; important aspect of who you are; supporting another person when difficult. Reapplicant essay additional. Optional information section (2000 chars).
- Resume: required — Upload resume; encouraged one page covering undergrad-and-after experiences.
- Transcripts: required — Upload transcript for every undergrad/grad school attended including grading scale; certified English translation if needed; transfer school transcripts required. Official via Re Vera after enrollment.
- Interview: by_invitation — All admitted applicants interview before offer. Guaranteed interview if complete app (incl. tests + LORs) by Sep 1 2026 (R1/C1) or Dec 1 2026 (R2/C2) 5pm ET; otherwise invitational. Virtual and limited on-campus; 30–45 min with Tuck Admissions Associates.
- Fee: $250 — waivers: Automatic one-time for listed groups (U.S. military active/veteran; recent Dartmouth/HACU/HBCU/TCU/Women’s College grads; UN LDCs; first-gen; Tuck undergrad programs; Discover Tuck visit attendees as of Jul 10 2026; etc.). Requestable with docs for Access Fellows, AmeriCorps, Forté, Fulbright, MLT, Pell, Peace Corps, TFA, etc.—≥2 business days before deadline. — Nonrefundable $250 USD; processing fees may apply.
- Other: Rounds: R1 Sep 24 2026 5pm ET; R2 Jan 5 2027; R3 Mar 24 2027 (+ Consortium Oct 15 2026 / Jan 5 2027)
- Other: Short-/long-term goals in Application Specifics (300 characters each)
- Other: International applicants encouraged R1/R2 for visa timing

GMAT/GRE required unless approved waiver before round-specific waiver deadline. Early complete apps can lock a guaranteed interview. No published English score floors—submit a solid DET/IELTS/TOEFL/PTE if not waived.
