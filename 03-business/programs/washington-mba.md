## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — GRE optional; GMAT 10th Edition, GMAT Focus, or GRE encouraged. GRE code 4854. Highest overall single-attempt score used; GMAT Superscore not incorporated for 2027 cycle. Test Optional path requires 'Test Optional Summary of Qualifications' (150 words max on analytical/quantitative preparedness). Optional ≠ waived requirement—applicants may apply without scores.
- GMAT: optional — GMAT optional (code 459-75-46 on requirements table; FAQ also lists 459-75-20—confirm live portal). EA not accepted for Full-time MBA. Test Optional available with short-answer evidence; internationals strongly encouraged to submit a score.
- TOEFL: required — min 5 — If native language is not English: TOEFL minimum 5 (100 iBT for tests before Jan 21, 2026). Code 4854. Official scores required before offer; valid 2 years on application submit date.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS 7.0 or higher (code 365). Duolingo 130+ also accepted (University of Washington Graduate Admissions).
- English waiver: Met via bachelor's/master's/doctoral degree from accredited institution with English as medium of instruction, subject to UW residency conditions for MOI degrees and documentation (except listed English-speaking countries). Living/working in U.S. alone does not satisfy. Requirement cannot be waived outside Policy 3.2 pathways.
- GPA: recommended — minimum 3 — scale 4.0 — No absolute minimum for Foster; GPA of 3.0 (B) or better is generally the standard for a competitive candidate. Report last 90 quarter / 60 semester graded credits on 4.0 scale per Grad School instructions. Below 3.0 reviewed holistically with test scores and other materials. UW Grad School Test Optional guidance also references ≥3.0 in most recent degree.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two recommendations required (up to three allowed). Preferably professional contacts. Short-answer questions plus written assessment (strengths, improvement areas, leadership, interpersonal skills, motivation, accomplishments).
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Required Essays (Post-MBA Plans; Foster Mission Statement) — Two required essays: (1) Post-MBA Plans 550–750 words; (2) Foster Mission Statement 350–550 words. Optional Essay (≤500 words) and Optional Nurturing Our Community Essay (350–550 words).
- Resume: required — Reverse-chronological work experience; up to three pages allowed; include education, activities, community involvement since high school.
- Transcripts: required — Upload scanned PDFs of original transcripts from completed four-year U.S. bachelor's (or equivalent) and other institutions attended. Include grading keys; English translation for non-English docs. Officials only after accepting offer.
- Interview: by_invitation — Required video interview invitation emailed 1–3 business days after round deadline (~1 week to complete). Separate admissions interview by invitation only (Zoom), typically 3–5 weeks after deadline; must complete if invited to remain under consideration.
- Fee: $90 — waivers: Foster does not offer program fee waivers; limited need-based waivers may be available via UW Graduate School. — Non-refundable $90 paid by credit card in online application.
- Other: 2026–27 cycle: R1 Oct 1 2026; R2 Jan 5 2027 (final for internationals); R3 Mar 23 2027. One application per academic year; fall intake only.
- Other: Consortium applicants supported; test optional available via Consortium as well.
- Other: Disclose AI assistance per Foster Integrity Statement if used to refine (not create) essays.

Test optional ≠ waived—still must complete Test Optional Summary. Round 2 is final international deadline. Official English scores must arrive by deadline.
