## CSC findings (one paragraph)
CSC’s 2026 flagship **High-Level University Graduate Program (SCH-067)** funds **PhD / joint PhD only** (living stipend + airfare; **no tuition**; foreign tuition waiver required). A broad national CSC overseas-**master’s** track has been largely closed since ~2019 (**SCH-071** research note). Verified master’s-capable CSC routes are narrow: **International Organization Talent (SCH-068)** (IO career track; tuition may be funded for degree master’s) and **Innovative Talent International Cooperation (SCH-083)** (unit project-system; limited master’s only if the approved project includes them; US only if partners listed). **Americas–Oceania cooperative scholarships (SCH-069)** 2026 public tables list AU/NZ/CA/MX partners — **no verified US MS/MBA line**. **Outstanding Self-Financed Students Scholarship (SCH-070)** is a mid/late-PhD one-time prize, not entry funding for Fall 2027 MS/MBA.
