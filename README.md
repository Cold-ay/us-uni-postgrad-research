# US postgraduate research — Fall 2027

Started: 16 October 2026 (Hong Kong). Status: research framework and initial source checks; scope clarified as a general applicant reference.

## Scope

Research Fall 2027 master's programs in computer science and business separately, at individual degree level. The purpose is to explain differences among universities and programs for a broad applicant audience, rather than produce a personalized admissions shortlist. Do not filter by budget or US location.

Cover taught, professional and research CS master's programs. For business, start with specialized master's (such as management, business analytics, finance and information systems); keep MBA comparisons in a separate category where included. Exclude PhDs from the main comparison. Do not presume that a department's reputation describes every degree it offers.

The user's background is computer science with internship experience and a Hong Kong / Taiwan connection. These are useful example applicant contexts, not restrictions on the audience. Exact citizenship and residency are unspecified; record Hong Kong and Taiwan scholarship eligibility separately and do not assume either from location or background.

## Comparison fields

For every program: exact degree/campus; duration; entry prerequisites; application and funding deadlines; THE/US News/QS ranking edition and category; tuition basis and total degree estimate; fees and living costs; faculty specialties and research/industry orientation; access to faculty and research; internship structure, support and outcomes; funding eligibility; and dated source links.

Evidence labels: **2027 confirmed**, **prior-year reference**, **undated/current page**, **inference**, **not verified**, **closed**. A missing result is not zero, ineligibility, or absence of opportunity. Record published date/academic year separately from access date. Do not average unlike ranking categories or treat ranking year as entry year.

School examples are source-validation seeds, not recommendations or a complete survey. Faculty profiles, placement outcomes, ranking positions and scholarship eligibility remain to be researched. Compare programs by applicant needs and documented characteristics rather than assigning universal best-school labels or personal admission odds.
