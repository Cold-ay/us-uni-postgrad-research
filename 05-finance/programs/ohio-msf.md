## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — GMAT or GRE optional (no preference). Scores valid 5 years. From Oct 1, 2024 only GRE taken at an ETS testing center accepted (not at-home); valid at-home GRE before that date still accepted. Apply without scores by answering YES to the no-score question.
- GMAT: optional — GMAT or GRE optional (no preference). Scores valid 5 years. Holistic review; test scores may strengthen application if submitted.
- TOEFL: required — min 79 — TOEFL iBT or Home Edition minimum 79 (4.0 on updated TOEFL launched Jan 2026). MyBest not accepted. Official scores ≤2 years old. ETS code 1592.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS Academic or Online minimum 7.0. One Skill Retake not accepted. Fisher international page lists 7.0 (university GPAdmissions general page lists 6.5).
- English waiver: Exempt if citizen of, or bachelor’s+ from, listed English-speaking countries/territories (Fisher international applicants page / gpadmissions.osu.edu). Duolingo also accepted (min 120). Degree from U.S. university also commonly cited for TOEFL waiver in Fisher FAQs.
- GPA: not_stated — No numeric minimum GPA stated on SMF applying page. Four-year bachelor’s (or equivalent) required; OSU generally does not treat 3-year bachelor’s as equivalent.
- Letters of recommendation: optional — Two references required (Admissions may contact). Formal letters of recommendation are optional; if supplied, recommenders email to FCOB-grad_apps@osu.edu with application ID. Apps may be reviewed before optional letters arrive.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Essay — Required essay: short- and long-term goals and how/why SMF helps achieve them. Recommended max 750 words. Optional supplemental essay (max ~500 words).
- Resume: required — Resume required; may exceed one page covering employment, extracurriculars, languages, military if applicable.
- Transcripts: required — Official transcripts from each undergrad/grad institution where credit earned (scanned official for review; verified electronic to GPADOCS@osu.edu or mailed). International: original language + certified English translation.
- Interview: required — Required Kira online recorded video interview for every applicant; application not reviewed until complete. Live interviews on a need-basis only.
- Fee: $60 — waivers: International applicants pay $70. — Nonrefundable $60 USD domestic (U.S. citizens/permanent residents/asylees); $70 international. Paid by credit card with online application.
- Other: No prerequisite courses; no post-undergrad work experience required (~75% of admits have <1 year).
- Other: Quantitative aptitude valued via coursework or GMAT/GRE.
- Other: AI policy: original voice required; limited AI for proofreading/brainstorming with disclosure; AI-generated content may disqualify.
- Other: Autumn 2027 application open; rolling review; priority/final deadlines posted on applying page.

Complete Kira video early; optional GMAT/GRE can help if quant background is thin. Apply early for merit aid (Priority 1 Dec 11, 2026).
