## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — Submission of GRE or GMAT optional per MSF admissions FAQ.
- GMAT: optional — Optional per MSF admissions page/FAQ.
- TOEFL: required — min 90 — Intl applicants: TOEFL or IELTS required unless USC English waiver. Grad Admission placement guidelines for master’s (not hard admit mins): TOEFL iBT ≥90 with ≥20 each section (pre-Jan 21 2026) or ≥4.5 with ≥4 each (new scale). Code 4852. Programs may set higher.
- IELTS: required — min 6.5 — IELTS Academic/Online: master’s placement guideline 6.5 overall with 6 each band. Select University of Southern California — Graduate Admission.
- English waiver: Exempt if degree from USC; bachelor’s from US or qualifying Anglophone country; completed master’s/doctoral from country where English is sole official language and language of instruction; or native English from listed countries. Not waived for English-taught programs in non-Anglophone countries or work experience.
- GPA: not_stated — No minimum GPA stated for standard MSF (PDP pathway notes ≥3.0 USC GPA for USC undergrads only).
- Letters of recommendation: not_stated — Standard MSF admissions criteria list does not include letters of recommendation (PDP pathway requires ≥1 from USC faculty).
- Statement of purpose: not_stated — Standard MSF admissions criteria list does not include a statement/essay (PDP requires ≤500-word essay).
- Resume: required — Resume listed among application criteria.
- Transcripts: required — Official transcripts from all colleges/universities attended. USC does not accept WES in lieu of official institutional records. Country-specific rules for foreign degrees.
- Interview: by_invitation — KIRA video interview by invitation only after application submitted.
- Fee: $175 — waivers: Not stated on MSF admissions page. — $175 application fee.
- Other: Fall start only
- Other: Intl strongly encouraged Round 1 or 2 (Round 3 decision not guaranteed for intl)
- Other: Financial docs + passport for I-20 after admission (not for admit decision itself per page notes)
- Other: STEM OPT extension eligible

Intl: apply Round 1/2 for visa; meet USC English policy or waiver. No LOR/essay on standard checklist—confirm in portal. Official scores/transcripts by deadline for timely decision.
