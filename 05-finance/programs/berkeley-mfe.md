## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — Require GMAT, GMAT Focus, GRE, or GRE General Test at home (valid 5 years; for Spring 2027 entry not older than Jan 1, 2022). Optional only if (a) PhD earned/will earn before MFE start, OR (b) U.S. four-year bachelor with final cumulative GPA ≥3.70/4.00 (no rounding; non-U.S. bachelor and non-4.0 scales ineligible). Committee may still request a score. GRE codes 4833 / dept 4399.
- GMAT: required — GMAT or GMAT Focus accepted interchangeably with GRE under same required/optional-eligibility rules above. Send to UC Berkeley Haas MFE.
- TOEFL: required — min 90 — TOEFL iBT/Home Edition minimum 90 (institution 4833, dept 82). MyBest and TOEFL ITP/Duolingo not accepted. Haas Knowledge also notes revised TOEFL scale ≥4.5 for exams after January 2026—confirm which scale applies to Spring 2028 entry when registering.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS Academic overall BAND ≥7; TOEFL preferred. Send TRF electronically to UC Berkeley Graduate Division.
- English waiver: Degree from country where official language is English; OR ≥1 year full-time coursework with grades B or better at a U.S. university (ESL/non-English/nonacademic/future courses excluded). Degrees from listed regions (e.g. India, PRC, Taiwan, most of Europe, etc.) do not meet country waiver even if instruction was English.
- GPA: required — minimum 3 — scale 4.0 — Satisfactory scholastic average, usually minimum 3.0 in upper-division work (Haas MFE requirements).
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two letters from academic or professional evaluators of quantitative ability. Co-workers/tutors/friends/family inadmissible. Professional email required (no free personal providers per Haas Knowledge).
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as essays — Series of essay questions in application; anti-plagiarism screening. Supplemental Information questions required (N/A if not applicable). Reapplicants within 2 years have special instructions.
- Resume: not_stated — Resume/CV not separately named on the requirements overview; work experience preferred but not required—confirm checklist in online application.
- Transcripts: required — Scans of official transcripts from all undergraduate and graduate programs attended. Degree from accredited institution before MFE start.
- Interview: by_invitation — Invitation-only; do not request. May cover prerequisites/resume topics; sometimes more than one interview. Interviewers are not the admissions committee.
- Fee: $275 — waivers: Not detailed on MFE requirements page beyond ETS GRE fee reduction partnership context. — Non-refundable $275 application fee (credit card preferred; check/money order to UC Regents).
- Other: Video essay required (webcam/mic; up to 2 attempts; ≤2 minutes).
- Other: Prerequisites: strong quant background (linear algebra, multivariate calculus, differential equations, numerical analysis, advanced stats/probability); programming exposure (C++, Python); stats/econometrics (e.g. R); ML concepts.
- Other: SPRING-ONLY start (January); no Fall admission. Full-time 1-year and Flex 2-year both spring start.
- Other: Spring 2028 rounds: Dec 1 2026; Jan 27; Apr 1; Jul 1 2027 (late to Jul 15 space-available).

Spring-only — do not list as Fall admit. Spring 2027 apps closed; Spring 2028 cycle opens later in 2026 per admissions hub.
