## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — Test scores not required for admission. Applicants may submit GRE to demonstrate potential; ASU does not accept GRE General Test at-home exam. Institution code 4007.
- GMAT: optional — Test scores not required. Optional GMAT may be submitted; W. P. Carey GMAT code 0GZ-1R-80.
- TOEFL: required — min 80 — Required for non-native English speakers. ASU graduate minimum: TOEFL iBT 80 if taken before Jan 21, 2026; minimum 4 on new scale if on/after Jan 21, 2026. W. P. Carey states its graduate minimums may be higher than ASU but does not publish a program-specific number on the MS-FIN page. Does not accept TOEFL iBT Home Edition if taken prior to Jan 21, 2026, Paper Edition, or Essentials.
- IELTS: required — min 6.5 — IELTS Academic overall band at least 6.5 (ASU graduate minimum). W. P. Carey may set higher unpublished program minimums.
- English waiver: ASU English proficiency exemptions (citizenship list, U.S. degree, medium-of-instruction, etc.) via Admission Services forms after applying. See admission.asu.edu English proficiency page.
- GPA: required — minimum 3 — scale 4.0 — ASU Degree Search: minimum cumulative GPA of 3.00 in last 60 semester / 90 quarter hours of first bachelor's, or 3.00 in applicable master's.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One letter of recommendation; provide recommender name, affiliation, and work email. Should comment on motivation, commitment, achievements, work experience, and likely success (Degree Search).
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Admission essay / short-answer question responses — Admission essay / short-answer question responses in graduate application. Personal statements will not be accepted and will delay review. Essay prompts are in the graduate application.
- Resume: required — Current resume must include positions held and clearly indicate dates of employment in Month/Year format.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial OK at application; if admitted, official transcripts from every college/university awarding bachelor's or higher required. ASU attendees need not submit ASU transcripts. International: original language + official English translation.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not mentioned on MS-FIN application-process page.
- Fee: $115 — waivers: Military and Citizen Service: U.S. Military, Teach for America, Peace Corps, or AmeriCorps — email wpcareymasters@asu.edu before applying. Domestic fee $70. — $70 domestic; $115 international. Non-refundable; applications not processed until fee received.
- Other: Bachelor's or master's from regionally accredited institution in business/management, economics, statistics, CS, or other STEM areas (Degree Search).
- Other: Three-year undergraduate degree with no additional coursework may qualify — contact wpcareymasters@asu.edu.
- Other: $500 non-refundable enrollment deposit after admission.

International priority Round II Feb 1, 2027; international final Round III Apr 1, 2027. Submit English proof and allow visa/financial guarantee time after admission.
