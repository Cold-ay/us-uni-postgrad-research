## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — Official GRE or GMAT required unless waived: 4-year U.S. undergrad GPA ≥3.7 OR Ph.D. Scores valid 5 years; higher quant score used if multiple. Institute GRE/TOEFL code 5248. Unofficial OK to evaluate; official needed before offer.
- GMAT: required — GMAT accepted (code HWK-54-37) but applicants with GMAT must apply through College of Business home unit. Same waiver rules as GRE.
- TOEFL: required — min 4.5 — TOEFL or IELTS only (Duolingo not accepted per QCF FAQ). GT Catalog: TOEFL iBT overall ≥4.5 (Speaking ≥3.5; Writing/Listening/Reading ≥4) on new scale; page notes prior iBT 90 ≈ 4.5. Also degree-from-approved-country or English-medium institution pathways.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS Academic overall 7.0 (Writing 5.5; Speaking/Listening/Reading 6.5) per GT Catalog.
- English waiver: GT Catalog approved methods: degree from approved-country institution; degree from English-medium institution (MOI letter may be required); USG campus ESL completion; or meeting TOEFL/IELTS minima. QCF international page highlights collegiate degree where English was sole official language of instruction.
- GPA: not_stated — No minimum GPA; holistic review with emphasis on quantitative coursework and quant test scores (QCF FAQ).
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 3 — Three recommenders (academic and/or professional). Minimum 2 of 3 needed for Grad Education to forward application.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as essay / short-answer questions — Essay ≤750 words on academic/professional motivation and post-degree career vision; plus short-answers (project ≤750; programming ≤500; optional weaknesses ≤250).
- Resume: required — Resume required (QCF Admissions FAQ).
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial transcripts uploaded; official may be required if admitted. International: native + English translations of transcripts and degree docs.
- Interview: by_invitation — Competitive candidates invited to ~30-minute behavioral Zoom interview with Associate Director (resume/projects/academic history).
- Fee: $95 — waivers: QCF states it does not provide application fee waivers. — $95 domestic / $105 international (e-check or credit card).
- Other: U.S.-equivalent 4-year bachelor’s required.
- Other: Quant background: Calc I–III, linear algebra, probability & statistics, differential equations; programming preferred (Python/R/C++/SAS).
- Other: Fall and Spring enrollment; Fall traditional ~1.5 yr with summer internship.
- Other: Fall 2027 deadlines: Early Oct 15 2026; Standard Dec 5 2026; Final intl outside US Mar 15 2027; Final US/PR/intl-in-US May 1 2027 (closes May 15).

GRE/GMAT required except U.S. 3.7+/PhD waiver. Duolingo not accepted.
