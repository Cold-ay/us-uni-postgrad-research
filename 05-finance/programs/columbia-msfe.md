## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — Self-reported valid GRE or GMAT required with application; no exceptions. Valid 5 years; submit ≥1 week before expiry. Official scores if admitted. GRE code 7432. Highest score used; no superscoring across sittings. Accepts GMAT 10th Edition and Focus.
- GMAT: required — GMAT or GRE required (no preference). MS Admissions GMAT code QF8-N6-46. Same validity/official-score rules as GRE.
- TOEFL: required — International students without bachelor’s/master’s/doctoral degree from English-instruction institution must take TOEFL, PTE, or IELTS. Self-report/scan with application; official if admitted. TOEFL ETS code 2141. No numeric minimum on requirements page. Scores must be valid at submission (typically 2 years per FAQ).
- IELTS: required — IELTS accepted; select Columbia Business School. No numeric minimum on requirements page.
- English waiver: Exempt only if degree from institution where English is the language of instruction; no exception requests accepted.
- GPA: not_stated — No minimum GPA. Class profile (entering 2025) average undergrad GPA 3.9 for context only.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two letters required from professors or managers who know your work; preferably one professor and one manager/supervisor. Up to four allowed. Applicant must not draft letters. Submit via online application.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Essays / short answer — MSFE: short answer — immediate post-MS professional goal (max 50 characters); Essay 1 — educational goals and academic/professional achievements most contributory to development (max 500 words); Essay 2 — expected career track and how MSFE helps attain goals, be specific (max 500 words).
- Resume: required — Resume or CV required covering academic credentials and relevant experience since high school (employment, campus paid/volunteer, other business/academic experience).
- Transcripts: required — Bachelor’s or equivalent required. Scan/PDF transcripts from each undergrad/grad institution attended; encrypted digital officials if admitted.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not listed on MS Application Requirements checklist.
- Fee: $100 — waivers: Active duty U.S. military; current Peace Corps, Teach for America, Teach for China, or Teach for India with proof of service. Email apply@gsb.columbia.edu after submitting application. — Nonrefundable US$100 via online application.
- Other: NOT the IEOR MFE — this is CBS MSFE.
- Other: Prerequisites (complete before program start at advanced undergrad level): Linear Algebra & Matrix Theory; two semesters Calculus (multivariable preferred); computer programming (Python preferred); Probability; Statistics; Macroeconomics preferred.
- Other: AI: idea generation/editing allowed; generating complete responses violates Honor Code.
- Other: MSFE deadline Jan 14, 2027 noon ET; applications open Aug 5, 2026 per deadlines page.

Ensure prereq coursework plan before matriculation. Distinguish CBS MSFE from Columbia IEOR MFE when researching.
