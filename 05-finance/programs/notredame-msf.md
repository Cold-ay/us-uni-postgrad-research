## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: optional — GMAT or GRE not required if applicant has or will have undergraduate degree from a U.S. college/university prior to enrollment. Applicants from colleges/universities outside the U.S. must submit valid GMAT or apply for GMAT waiver. Scores ≤5 years. GRE code 6998.
- GMAT: optional — Not required for U.S. bachelor's holders/expected graduates. Required for non-U.S. institution applicants unless waiver granted via waiver form. GMAT code XM7-2D-68.
- TOEFL: required — Non-U.S. citizen applicants (and U.S. citizens with extensive international study) must submit English proficiency. Mendoza MSF page accepts TOEFL; does not publish a minimum score. Mendoza TOEFL code 2456. ND Graduate School publishes TOEFL iBT 80 (pre-Feb 2026; Speaking ≥23) or 4.5 avg / no section <4.0 (updated) — cite Grad School separately; confirm with Mendoza if program-specific floor differs.
- IELTS: required — IELTS accepted on Mendoza MSF page; minimum not published there. ND Graduate School lists IELTS 7.0 composite.
- English waiver: May be waived if completed at least six full-time semesters or nine full-time quarters at a university where English is the primary language (transcripts verify; committee may request more).
- GPA: not_stated — No minimum GPA stated on MSF apply page.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 1 — One recommendation; someone familiar with professional or academic background who can offer detailed assessment.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Written statement — Written statement ≤500 words: detailed post-master's career plans; how background + Notre Dame studies equip you; short- and long-term goals with industry and function.
- Resume: required — One-page resume summarizing education, work experience, skills, accomplishments.
- Transcripts: required — From all colleges/universities attended including summer and graduate study. International transcripts need official English translation. Unofficial OK for application; official final with proof of graduation before enrollment if admitted. Degree audits not accepted.
- Interview: required — Video assessment required after application submission (Admissions Committee questions); must complete to move forward. Not framed as optional invitation-only live interview.
- Fee: $50 — waivers: U.S. Active Duty Military or Veterans; current/former Peace Corps; current/former Teach for America — contact msf.business@nd.edu. — $50 non-refundable; pay via application status page after submit. Application incomplete until paid.
- Other: Applicant Snapshot: required 4-slide PDF deck (no audio/video).
- Other: Optional supplemental essay for additional context.
- Other: Also accepts PTE Academic and Duolingo for English (mins not on Mendoza MSF page).

International applicants strongly encouraged to apply by Round 2 for visa timing. Non-U.S. undergrad degree holders: plan GMAT or GMAT waiver early.
