## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — GRE or GMAT required (waivers available). Scores within past five years. ETS/GRE code 4053. Waiver paths include business bachelor's from AACSB/EQUIS school; BS in finance/accounting/business analytics/STEM; ≥4 units stats/calculus B+ or better; certain professional licenses; 5+ years quantitative work; partner-institution alumni — email admissions@cgu.edu when application started.
- GMAT: required — GMAT or GRE required with same waiver policy. GMAT code DZK-QS-08.
- TOEFL: required — min 95 — Non-native English speakers: TOEFL minimum 95 (old scale) / 5 (new score scale) for direct entry. Code 4053. Lower scores may enter via International Scholars Program (TOEFL 75/4).
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS 7.0 with no sub-score less than 6.5 for direct entry. Also PTE 66; Duolingo 125 for direct entry.
- English waiver: Citizens/PRs of English sole-official-language countries; degree from U.S. or those countries; recent U.S. academic English pre-master's/bridge; or degree where English is a primary language of instruction for majority of courses with documentation.
- GPA: not_stated — No GPA minimum published on MSF page or Drucker 2027 how-to-apply news (Associate Dean: no score cutoffs / no GPA minimums).
- Letters of recommendation: optional — Optional. If submitting, recommender emails letter to admissions@cgu.edu with applicant full name. News post: no longer required.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Applicant questionnaire — Applicant questionnaire: five short responses ≤100 words each (motivation; goals; alignment with Drucker ideals; contribution; biggest challenge). Upload to status page after online application.
- Resume: required — Up-to-date resume required.
- Transcripts: required — Sealed official transcript from every undergrad/grad institution that granted a degree; electronic to admissions@cgu.edu OK. Unofficial for review; official upon admission. Bachelor's from regionally accredited college required.
- Interview: required — Required for admission upon review of application; decision not issued until interview complete. Drucker news: every applicant who completes an application is interviewed.
- Fee: $80 — $80 non-refundable (university requirements on MSF page).
- Other: Prerequisite: Financial Accounting (coursework B or better at accredited institution or approved equivalent) before entering.
- Other: Fall 2027: priority Feb 1, 2027; international final June 1, 2027 (program page) / June 5, 2027 (Drucker news table — slight date conflict; verify closer to cycle).
- Other: STEM designated.

International final deadline earlier than domestic for visa. Reach out to Drucker recruiters early; interview is mandatory after complete file.
