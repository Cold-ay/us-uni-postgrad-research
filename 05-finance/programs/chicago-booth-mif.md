## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: required — Valid GMAT or GRE required; no preference. Test waiver if current UChicago undergraduate or graduated from a UChicago program. Current students in UChicago graduate programs NOT eligible for waiver.
- GMAT: required — GMAT or GRE required except UChicago undergrad/alumni waiver (not for current UChicago grad students).
- TOEFL: required — International non-native English speakers: TOEFL, IELTS Academic, or PTE. TOEFL code 1832-02. Scores valid 2 years. At-home/online proctored accepted. No numeric minimum published on how-to-apply page.
- IELTS: required — IELTS Academic accepted; no numeric minimum on how-to-apply page.
- English waiver: Exempt if undergrad/postgrad degree from listed countries (incl. Antigua & Barbuda, Australia, Bahamas, Barbados, Canada, Dominica, Grenada, Guyana, Hong Kong, Ireland, Jamaica, New Zealand, St. Kitts & Nevis, St. Lucia, St. Vincent & Grenadines, Singapore, South Africa, Trinidad & Tobago, UK, US); OR degree outside those countries with English as only language of instruction; OR living and working full-time in U.S. ≥2 years. Citizenship alone does not exempt. Committee may still require a score.
- GPA: not_stated — No minimum GPA stated. Holistic review of background, experiences, grades, and tests.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two letters from people who know you well with specific examples of academic performance/contributions; prefer substance over title. Online form only (no mail/fax).
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Essay questions + video essay — Two essays: (1) How Booth MiF helps immediate and long-term career goals (max 300 words); (2) Something about who you are outside classroom/work—activity pursued for its own sake (max 400 words). Optional clarification section. Plus required video essay ≤60 seconds introducing yourself and a non-career pursuit.
- Resume: not_stated — Resume/CV not listed on MiF How to Apply checklist.
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial scans of all postsecondary transcripts (both sides) with application; official transcripts upon admission. Non-English records need official record + authorized English translation.
- Interview: by_invitation — Invitation-only after application evaluation; 100% virtual with admissions staff; required for admission (interview ≠ guarantee). Also brief video simulation exercise with interview invite.
- Fee: How-to-apply checklist does not state an application fee amount; confirm in online application system.
- Other: Recommended advanced quantitative coursework and coding exposure (e.g., Linear Algebra, Statistics, Multivariable Calculus, Python, C++).
- Other: Early Review from Oct 1, 2026 (rolling to mid-Dec); Round One Jan 7, 2027.
- Other: Submit by 11:59 p.m. CT on deadline day.

Prepare for video essay and possible interview simulation. UChicago undergrads/alumni should claim test waiver; others must submit GMAT/GRE.
