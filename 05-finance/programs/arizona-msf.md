## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: waived — GMAT/GRE requirement waived for Fall 2027 (stated on admissions page Application Strengths and Required Materials). Historical note on page also describes alternate waiver paths (Finance/Accounting/STEM degree with ≥3.3 GPA, CFA levels, Charter)—but Fall 2027 policy is explicit waiver.
- GMAT: waived — GMAT (or GRE) waived Fall 2027. School code 4832 if somehow submitting. Page still mentions older waiver alternatives; treat Fall 2027 as waived.
- TOEFL: required — min 100 — International / non-native: Eller FAQ requires iBT TOEFL ≥100. Score <2 years old at first MSF class unless living/studying in U.S. since test. Code 4832.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS overall ≥7.0 with ≥6.0 each section (Eller FAQ / UA Grad College).
- English waiver: Waived if completed a four-year bachelor’s from a U.S. college/university and have not returned home for an extended period (FAQ); also if completed a degree program in the U.S. Check country requirements on UA Graduate College site.
- GPA: required — minimum 3 — scale 4.0 — Minimum GPA 3.00. UA Grad College enforces ≥3.0 on last 60 undergrad units or 12 grad credits (rare exception >2.95 with strong credentials).
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — 2 Letters of Reference: names and contact information. Academia or industry; should address study/quantitative skills.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Statement of Purpose — Statement of Purpose: academic/professional goals in finance; typically 1–2 typed double-spaced pages to Finance Department Admissions Committee.
- Resume: required — Resume required in application package.
- Transcripts: required — Transcripts from all colleges/universities except UA (include grade key if not 4.0 scale). International: original language + English translation; official sealed.
- Interview: unknown — Interview not listed among Required Application Materials.
- Fee: $90 — waivers: UA Graduate College does not waive application fees (general email requests not considered). — UA Graduate College degree-seeking application fee $90 nonrefundable.
- Other: No work experience required.
- Other: Prefer B.S./B.A. in business-related field; other fields may need summer FinPrep/prereq modules.
- Other: International: financial guarantee; affidavit of domicile for AZ residents.
- Other: Fall deadlines include Oct 15 early; May 15 international; Aug 1 final.

Fall 2027 GMAT/GRE waived—do not delay for a test. Non-finance majors should plan FinPrep if notified.
