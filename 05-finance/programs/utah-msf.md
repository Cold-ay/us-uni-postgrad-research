## Application requirements

As of 2026-10-27.

- GRE: not_required — Official admissions requirements and FAQ list do not include GRE/GMAT; not listed among required materials.
- GMAT: not_required — Not listed among required admissions materials on MSF admissions or FAQ pages.
- TOEFL: required — min 90 — Intl: TOEFL iBT min 90 (pre-Jan 21 2026) or 5 (on/after Jan 21 2026). Program mins higher than general Admissions Office.
- IELTS: required — min 7 — IELTS Academic (in-person) minimum 7.0.
- English waiver: Exemptions per University of Utah Admissions Office site. Duolingo English Test min 120 also accepted. Official score required before final decision.
- GPA: required — minimum 3 — scale 4.0 — Bachelor’s with cumulative undergraduate GPA ≥3.0 from accredited college/university (or equivalent). Optional essay recommended if below.
- Letters of recommendation: required — count 2 — Two letters required (not family/friends). Prefer professional if FT experience; faculty/advisors/internship OK. Fast Track (GPA ≥3.5 + prereqs B+) and Express Admit can forgo LORs.
- Statement of purpose: required — also known as Written essays / video essay — Two short essays 300–750 words: (1) overcoming adversity; (2) why MSF and career goals. Optional essay for weaknesses. Plus required video essay ≤2 minutes. Express Admit: no written essays/LORs if apply by Oct 31, 2026.
- Resume: required — Chronological career history with dates, duties; education and certifications; international travel for school/work/residence (not vacations).
- Transcripts: required — Unofficial from all HE institutions at apply (must show name, courses, grades, GPAs). Official after admission. Intl: NACES course-by-course eval (university covers cost after English score received).
- Interview: unknown — Live interview not listed as required on admissions page; required video essay. Scholarship page notes interviews may factor scholarship decisions.
- Fee: $65 — waivers: Not stated on MSF admissions page. — $55 domestic / $65 international; non-refundable.
- Other: Prerequisites (B or better) before admit decision: Intro Accounting + Financial Management (or Series 7 / CFA L1 for finance prereq); Sophia Learning alternatives
- Other: Fall 2027: Priority Feb 1 2027; Intl Apr 1 2027; Scholarship Jun 1; Final Aug 1
- Other: Fast Track and Express Admit pathways

Intl: Apr 1 2027 intl deadline for Fall 2027 F-1; allow time for transcript eval + official English. Complete prereqs early. Hybrid/evening options exist.
